TheCore on one Svalboard hand

The left hand is on the Svalboard, the right hand is on an ordinary mouse, so the whole of TheCore has to fit on 20 left finger keys plus a layer held under the thumb Nail. This mapping is computed, not hand-picked: tools/thecore_svalboard.py orders TheCore's keys by how often they fire in 187 pro replays (wiki/sc2-command-sequences.md), blended 50/50 with per-minute rates averaged equally across the 18 co-op commanders of coop-summary.json, the co-op side normalized to equal total load before the mix (--coop-blend 0.5 --coop-normalize), places each one on the easiest free key well its finger role allows, then swaps pairs while that lowers same-finger work. Rules and citations are in wiki/thecore-method-on-a-svalboard.md sections 4a-4d. The drawing is schematic: real key wells are cupped clusters, not flat squares.
