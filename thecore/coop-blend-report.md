# What the 50/50 co-op blend costs 1v1 play (issue #27)

Generated by `tools/coop_blend_report.py`. Blend: each key's load =
0.5 x 1v1 per-minute rate + 0.5 x co-op rate (equal-commander mean
over 18 commanders, rates averaged, never pooled); the bigram rates
that feed the same-finger cost blend the same way. Constraints,
finger weights, slot order and the optimizer are unchanged.

Cost units are events per minute: same-finger different-key
transition rate (cross-plane at half weight) plus load times slot
difficulty. The same-finger figures below are that first term.
