# What the co-op blends cost 1v1 play (issue #27)

Generated by `tools/coop_blend_report.py`. Three layouts per file:

* **1v1-only**: the layout of main, load and bigrams from the 187
  IEM replays alone.
* **raw 50/50**: each key's load = 0.5 x 1v1 per-minute rate +
  0.5 x co-op rate (equal-commander mean over 18 commanders, rates
  averaged, never pooled); bigram rates blend the same way. Co-op
  per-key rates total ~5x below 1v1 (bigram totals ~10x below),
  so this gives co-op well under half the influence.
* **normalized 50/50** (`--coop-normalize`, the committed layout on
  this branch): the co-op load dict is scaled by (sum of 1v1 key
  loads / sum of co-op key loads) and the co-op pair dict by the
  analogous pair-total ratio before the 50/50 mix, so the two
  corpora contribute equal total mass.

Constraints, finger weights, slot order and the optimizer are
unchanged throughout. Cost units are events per minute: same-finger
different-key transition rate (cross-plane at half weight) plus
load times slot difficulty. The headline is the normalized layout's
1v1 regression.
