# Learning and practice

Message ids in parentheses are the last 6 digits of Discord snowflakes in `discord/raw/channel-389438169520799746.jsonl`; a few 6-digit suffixes collide in the 23k-message export, so disambiguate by date if a lookup returns two hits. Agreement counts are floor estimates.

## Whether to switch at all

- **New to SC2: switch immediately.** No muscle memory to override, and it spares the mechanical wall that forces a relearn at some rank (937189); click as little as possible, look at the hotkey then press it (825007, 024361). Hotkeys before the campaign, which is then only for unit names (172366) — except v6 is multiplayer-only, so campaign units are unbound (355020); others learned units "by dying to it", or from pro games and liquipedia (786503, 277334).
- **Since 2023: start on TheCore rather than learn the defaults first.** The first couple of hours are worse, everything after is better, and switching later costs a couple of weeks (361694, 171153). Through 2022 most already said go direct rather than via grid (124801, 777828, 216165), Poletes more cautious (740728); the 2021–2022 alternative was a ladder of standard -> Core-Lite -> Core 6, starting fresh being easier than relearning (713725, 712916).
- **The switch is optional and personal.** Poletes' test: worth it if optimizing hotkeys excites you, if you feel RSI, or if you are new (871623); most players at all skill levels do not use the Core, and skipping it will not stop you reaching GM (013387). JaKaTaK, 2019: a very personal decision, go for it if you have the 20-40 hours and it is worth it to you (620224). For casual players, grid beats standard and the Core may delay your enjoyment (647131, 556171).
- **From grid it is easier than from standard** (594119); standard's mnemonics fail anyway if you do not know unit names (839677). The pitch to grid users: most-used functions under resting fingers, 8 cameras and 10 control groups more accessible than 1-0 plus F3-F8, less finger repetition (607700, 149201); the hand-health argument is the thumb modifiers (861481, 878529).
- **Switch everything at once, "rip the bandaid off"** (965884). **It does not damage touch typing** — asked once (671647), answered no by five people (681395, 470794, 221544, 773662).
- A version jump is smaller than the first switch: standard to core5 about a month, 5 to 6 far faster (893765, 181246), since the hard part is the right side of the keyboard and that transfers (007898).

## How long it takes

- **Since 2022: weeks to months, not a week.** "somewhere from a few weeks to a few months" (944263, 564948); at 1-2 hours a day, back where you were after 4-6 weeks (976332). Restated as a week of hard play or a month of light play (712327), one or two weeks of "1000 ms brain ping" (310997), two to three weeks before you feel like you are really playing (968197); CoreLite is 10-15 minutes plus a few days (718850, 179230).
- **Through 2021 estimates were shorter, a week or two of drills then ladder.** Two weeks of drills first (542720); a week hard or four weeks casual (051780); two weeks at three hours a week, the first week or two genuinely unpleasant (621259, 451793); a week off games then two more (044392); one week plus a second on team games (686972); a couple of weeks back to your old APM, laddering within days (805960, 384546); a week or two of a few games a day (891571). Outlier: 10 hours following the Course (751839).
- **Tiered version, 2021**: 20 hours to have it down, 100 to navigate confidently, 10,000 to master (273858); or two weeks to comfortable, months to your old level, at 1-2 hours a day (200003).
- **Correct repetitions, not calendar time, are the unit.** JaKaTaK: muscle memory "takes the same number of correct repetitions to develop as anything else, like learning a scale on a musical instrument" (325048). Knowing where everything is took two weeks; understanding why it is arranged that way, two months (132083).

## Adaptation symptoms

- **Worse before better, and the gains land late** — the layout will "make you slightly physiologically unhappy" (530463), "a lot of your progress magically manifests the day after you practice" (717952), worse short term and better long term (279958). The first week or two is awkward (073906), or "hell" (302076).
- **A sore pinky in the first weeks is normal and passes** (968197, 872879); pinky fatigue runs roughly 40 to 60 hours of deliberate practice (428574).
- **Fat-fingering is a normal phase** (838140), but the layout "will require precision training - it is not fat-fingering friendly" (684608). APM collapses first: 250 to 65 in three games (918151).

## Accuracy before speed

- **Go slow enough not to make mistakes; accuracy produces speed.** The channel's oldest and most repeated rule (507072, 348402, 162044, 645066, 999394, 721067) — "one wrong button press hurts you a lot more in creating muscle memory" (343303).
- **Wrong reps actively un-learn.** If a sequence takes 200 reps, a wrong one "not only doesn't get you closer to that 200 but it also unlearns part of that muscle memory" (608626); against no opponent, do everything correctly on the first try no matter how long it takes (816147).
- **Do not ladder while fumbling hotkeys** — customs or AI until control groups are ~90% fluent: "issues of being bad are fine to take to ladder, but issues of fluency mean you haven't learned the hotkeys yet" (499129, 975309). Unranked shares the ranked MMR pool, so it does not protect your rating (147310).
- **To kill one specific misclick, play two unranked games at half APM** thinking only about the right key (100861) — the fix for wiping control groups instead of pressing `m`, which the chart gives to the pinky (698205, 436122).
- **Play with a metronome**: a beep every 10 to 30 seconds, restart the macro checklist on each beep, shorten the interval, then drop it (427612, 629780).
- **Never skip cameras or control groups** — "a terrible habbit to break at a later point" (680192, 014482).
- **Drill deliberately rather than laddering** — the same defense 100 times, because "no matter how many ladder games I play, I still look at the keyboard to find that key" (788772); PiG is named as one of the few streamers teaching mechanics separately (856488).
- **Measure one thing**, e.g. worker counts at 3, 4, 5 and 6 minutes against a pro replay of the same build (537301).

## The Course

- **"The Course", the step-by-step spreadsheet in the Google Drive folder, is the standard answer to "where do I start", 2019 through 2024** (534249, 802123, 371595, 390277, 933916, 446096, 674252, 189642, 395975, 596160, 779850, 302743, 225863). Neither race- nor version-specific, so it needs no per-version update (315099, 173471), and it is the standard advice for adapting to a new version too (871622); it has had many updates over the years (069451).
- **Order**: build every building, produce every unit, use every spell; then the opening sequence; then vs AI for build orders — "mind numbingly boring, but... very efficient" (916166). Jump build drill and opening sequence come before location hotkeys (043734). Startup and camera sequence is section 3.0, also in the FAQ channel (525002, 970635, 060352, 450944, 163680).
- **2023 refinement: do steps 0-3, skip 4 and 5, do step 6 against no opponent first** (447680, 717133). **"Ghosting"** = touch but do not press the key that would select the unit, then press the key asked for, so you learn whole action sequences (407362).
- **Write down on paper which building type and spellcaster goes on each control group before starting** (819729), and **turn off simple command card** so every hotkey shows on screen (934912).
- **Edennil's method** (199744): memorize ability key order from the "Buildings Units Spells" tab, since abilities are always added left to right in the same order; drill the first five or six; 10-15 minutes a day on the hotkey trainer; four or five control groups at first; then slow customs vs AI.
- **Laaxus's five steps** (649980), reported working (199524): opening sequence in a custom game, which teaches the camera hotkeys; hotkey trainer for units and buildings; AI until you beat Elite; unranked; ranked.
- **Dissent, 2020:** the Course is "a bit lacking" and the handbook "reads more like developer notes than actual instruction", so its generality forces you to come ask (899943, 187134). The conceded point was no signposting by player level (295641); one member wanted a roadmap from downloading the game to multiplayer (334623).

## Reading the layout

- **The second page of the main spreadsheet is a learning tool, not just a reference**: suggested control groups, plus the ideal finger beside each key (912391, 477698, 237093). Camera drills and control-group usage are on the "Control Groups Cameras" tab (534249, 802123); directed drills live on a separate drills spreadsheet (951712, 745590).
- **Look up the finger chart rather than guessing** — Drive, "information and data sheets", "finger chart" (987688, 313366).
- **Learn building positions from the graphics, not the letters**; the spatial relationships are what to internalize (831911).
- **Control group numbers are meaningless; think in keys.** CG 1-10 map to O, I, L, K, 9, 0, 8, U, comma, period in no order — "There is the control group on O, the control group on I…" (531614); renumber to suit yourself (359873).
- **Ability keys are the easy part** — everything is on either K or P depending whether it is rapidfire; control groups, cameras and control-group stealing are the hard part (861632, 639518). The design goal to keep in mind is no double-tapping: "it is faster to press pk than to press pp" (708789).
- **The logic only lands after the muscle memory.** Bose, learning protoss on Core, found placements intuitive in hindsight — high-tech unit on the far key, flying production on advanced+key — but "that sort of thing requires you to learn -> get the experience before the logic makes sense" (566291). He wanted the docs to mark what is vital vs open to change; Edennil rejected the framing — all of it is optional, every decision is a tradeoff, and listing tradeoffs for every key is unrealistic — while agreeing a common-customizations section was always the goal (838696, 838451, 021150, 158820, 287785, 051658). See [layouts-and-variants](/layouts-and-variants.md).

## The Hotkey Trainer arcade map

- **Current advice: the arcade map "Hotkey Trainer", newest patch version, one or two minutes a day, stop when bored — then a Very Easy AI custom game running your build slowly but perfectly** (370814, 387531). It has been the first drill anyone is pointed at since 2019 (066522, 820774, 452172, 712715, 460968, 052372, 904661, 083925, 256539, 806780, 038898, 139028).
- **Custom -> Arcade, not Melee.** Names seen: "Hotkey Trainer LotV Patch 4.12.0" (355765, 431814, 608691), "Hotkey Trainer LotV 4.1.4" (541020), "Hotkey Trainer LoTv 4.7" (370814). Links posted: `battlenet:://starcraft/map/1/304464` (151885) and the 2023 NA copy `battlenet:://starcraft/map/1/348920` (440320); no EU copy found.
- **The number is the SC2 patch, not a map version — take the newest patch number, not the highest-looking one** (708767, 094144, 841660, 407713), or the last entry in the search list (819551). Maintainers: Bitstorm originally, Justice41 for 4.11 (832704), Kethryes for 4.12.0 (663892).
- **It reads your own hotkey file, so it is layout-independent** (931280) — but some copies run older SC2 data and a few keys look wrong (979840).
- **Two traps.** It deliberately unbinds abilities so you must look them up and rebind inside the map — one player thought spine/spore uproot was missing from 6.0 and it was the trainer (024603, 417232) — and its SCV building keys are wrong by design because the map uses its own: just rebind (742996, 100436).
- **Switch mode by selecting the building, or pylon, on the right of the map** to drill buildings and spells rather than only units; one member thought it only drilled SCV building (494538, 092648, 336264, 021364). Attack-move, hold, stop and patrol drills are missing entirely (021364).
- **The stated target is 200 correct in a row without mistakes** (088433). Pokebunny hit that on units, buildings and researches in two days but could not manage over ten minutes at a time (722611); Mell00yell00 calls it "the hyperbolic time chamber" and spent six hours smoothing out the armory (822909); jaydik, "boring af" but a big speed-up (261756, 640854, 740723), grind it with a beer and music then go to customs (215785); NecroDae at three days, "the hotkey trainer is doing wonders" (400799).
- **Limits.** Siaal: half an hour was enough, he would rather build things in customs (811073). It drills keys in isolation, not the real sequence camera -> SCV -> build -> barracks -> click ground (059870), so simulate whole control-group sequences in it (323028) and mix ~15 minutes of trainer with real customs (547432, 861578).
- **It goes stale between SC2 patches**: outdated 2019-12 while 6 settled (941696, 311852), out of date 2020-11 (555550, 073738), several things undoable by 2022 (756329, 578118), not matching the current file in 2022-09 (806960, 154877). The 4.11 map was for a while the only one not still asking for infested terran (424622, 187476). This class of map is what corrupted possum's hotkey file — see [hotkey-file-editing](/hotkey-file-editing.md) (066011).

## Custom games and the quit-and-rewind loop

- **The standing loop: a custom game against no or very easy AI, one build you know, run slowly, then "Quit & Rewind" and take command instead of remaking the lobby** (684809, 071552). After surrendering, click "rewind" instead of surrender, then "Resume from Replay" (665310); fuller — esc, quit/rewind, drag the slider bottom-right to the start, pause, "resume from replay" (296222, 797665, 664916, 323206, 802432, 450098). Called "the most important thing ever" (861204); three people reacted with surprise it exists (228168, 580370, 453376). Siaal's earlier form — play, surrender, rewind, take control — fails if your default replay interface is WCS or GameHeart (761115, 986374).
- **No AI at all, or very easy**, so you can play slowly enough to always hit the right key (721354, 905099); Neuro also recommends empty games (581352). Counter: very easy beats empty, because an empty game triggers instant victory and you cannot practice the worker split (296222) — on a 1v1 map with no opponent, take "return to game" after the instant win (071552). Poletes' stricter 2020 line was no AI at all while still learning keys (522951).
- **Drill one build to perfection against easiest AI, then splitting** — around the 5-7 minute mark split the army into 2-3 groups and move them around the map (655529).
- **Low-pressure alternatives**: co-op (692759), campaign (982604) — though v6 is multiplayer-only, so campaign units are unbound (355020) — and for protoss, customs against players ~200 MMR below (278796). Back up the hotkey file before campaign practice; see [hotkey-file-editing](/hotkey-file-editing.md).

## Fingers

- **Always hit a given key with the same finger; "that is part of what touch typing does"** (813830, 146016). `/` is the only key that legitimately alternates, thumb or index (457556).
- **`/` is a thumb key in practice** — Edennil "100% of the time", jaydik "I am 100% thumb", against an official "thumb or index depending on context" (476428, 639465). `.` is middle if you tilt, middle or index if square (330110); `m` is always pinky in the chart (698205, 436122).
- **Comfort and accuracy beat the chart** — do what is comfortable, while using the proper finger while learning (486740); the guides exist to cut wrist rotation (762132).
- **The charts are imperfect**: both circulating ones are slightly out of date, the second closer, no updated one exists (219658, 881960); the chart people mistake for the right hand is the left (838016). As of 2021-03 they lack a colour key and do not mark home keys, the old TeamLiquid chart being preferred, and the complaint was conceded (102026, 223060, 294622).
- **The right hand does the work** — the left thumb is vestigial while the right thumb reaches all modifiers and the right pinky picks up extra keys (548018); the right side is the hard part and the skill that transfers between versions (007898).

## Home position and physical technique

- **Home position on 6.0 is JIOP, bump on `J`, thumb on Control** (757736, 750377, 326259, 819956). Army is primary `J`, secondary `L`, then M/H/N (572180); double-tap `J` to select and center (891678), preferred over Alt-centering for the lower error rate (700265). Tapping Alt to jump cameras is technically better but almost nobody sustains it — Poletes knows one person who did (153903, 850482).
- **Simplified start for a beginner struggling with `,` and `.`**: keep the fingers on JKL — "j and l are army groups, k has important abilities, and ; is amove. other abilities are on p and / which are one away from ;" (620038, 309185). Other fingers moving with the pressed one is normal (906118).
- **Fake the F/J home bumps** with tape (245120) or a paper-clip snip glued to `;` (340783). **Do not tilt the keyboard much for 6.0** — a learner worked this out himself and found `;` far more accessible after (843723, 291664).
- **Thumb-shift trouble is common, and usually a hand-position problem.** Bend the thumb knuckle so it does not jam its neighbours (799902, 161256); for shift without control, thumb tip at about 120 degrees, ctrl and list key with the thumb joint (256155). Edennil's fix was rotating the board clockwise after pulling the win/fn keycap (274576, 020945, 754673); bluChEEZ removed the menu key and made Enter a third modifier (017436). The thumb rests near shift, and v6's original resting key was shift, per the HomeKeys and SpeedZones image (143370). See [keyboards-and-hardware](/keyboards-and-hardware.md).

## Control groups and cameras while learning

- **Use cameras and control groups from the very first game**; skipping them builds a habit that is hard to undo (806780, 038898). But not all 10 at once (020682) — four or five first (199744).
- **Overlayed cameras, two per key, are a memory problem, not a mechanical one**: v1-v5 put every camera behind a modifier and nobody complained; only v6's doubled-up keys draw complaints (010970).
- **Zerg cameras, from Poletes**: the first 7 on bases, set even if you never take 7 (802251); none on creep tumors, since creep moves too fast — use the minimap — and none on micro, double-tap the control group to center instead (309525, 859742). Rally-cam uses for zerg, who have no true rally point: an 8th base, the opponent's natural for cheap scouting, their main when cheesing, or a nydus exit (107425, 382475). Opening camera placement is covered in detail (643112, 530816).
- **Terran macro groups**: CCs on one and all production on another; or rax, factory and starport on three keys so they rally separately; or both, best but slowest to set up (412481, 424415). Zerg needs 2-3 — hatches, inject queens, creep queens (378580) — with the 6.0 convention `i` inject queens, `o` hatcheries, `g` or `h` creep queens and defense, only `o` mandatory (489994, 745216).
- **Removing units from a control group**: select it, ctrl+click the wireframes you want gone, press the trash hotkey, `,` by default (668682, 930496).
- **Buffer your first worker by holding P through the loading screen** — eight cameras, a drone and a hatchery hotkeyed before the first overlord (827453, 191450).

## Zerg macro drills

- **Learn the macro cycle as one unit: inject bases, build units, build overlords, spread creep, every 30 seconds.** jaydik, crediting day[9]: 4-5 seconds once fast, leaving 25 for micro (822087, 188000, 254164); short form, inject, build, creep, repeat (885416, 705610).
- **The inject sequence is the reason to be on 6.** Cameras on `0 9 8 U`, four inject queens on `i`: `I - 0 - / - click - 9 - / - click - 8 - / - click - U - / - click` (209371, 432804). General form: select queen hotkey, press inject, cam 1, click, press inject, cam 2, click — how Serral, Lambo and Reynor inject (198388). Edennil's fingering, hand shifted slightly up: "0 with index / `/` with thumb / click / 9 with middle / `/` with thumb / click / 8 with ring / ... / U with pinky" (682048, 272801).
- **Use `/` on the thumb, not `;` on the index** — `;` repeats the index finger when jumping to camera 1 (466651), and is why new 6g zergs attack-move their own hatchery (303879). Index suits shift-queueing or box injecting, thumb the camera-hop cycle (931457, 102120); the recommended method is a linked video (021200).
- **Do not shift-queue injects** — Poletes' repeated warning, because queued injects send queens wandering across the map (094058, 116241, 510073). Click the hatchery rather than double-tapping the inject key (754322, 255361).
- **Camera locations matter far more than whether queens are hotkeyed**: both inject methods work, and hotkeying the queens is small in comparison (473154). Order 0-9-8-U versus U-8-9-0 is about a 50/50 split, neither taught as correct (252416, 700787).
- **Inject when the lowest-energy queen hits 25.** jaydik "sacrifices perfection for ease of repetition" and cycles `0 9 8` regularly anyway (110185). Edennil's fuller model: box-inject each queen at 25 energy early, switch to control group plus camera in midgame once energies converge, then either move creep queens onto the inject hotkey and cycle twice, or mass shift-queue them into one hatchery (660068, 970354); some builds stack injects deliberately so queens can be pulled for defense (977872).
- **Egg hotkeying, Method A vs Method B**, is written out in full (920074, 932800, 250153).
