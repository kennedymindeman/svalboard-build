# Keyboards and hardware

Message ids in parentheses are the last 6 digits of Discord snowflakes in `discord/raw/channel-389438169520799746.jsonl`; a few 6-digit suffixes collide in the 23k-message export, so disambiguate by date if a lookup returns two hits. Agreement counts are floor estimates.

## Board size and form factor

- **A 60% works, and has since 2021.** No F-row key is used; the F-row reaches menus only and can go on a layer (353006, 877598, 300865, 128474, 139658, 930445, 221936, 116810, 220393); Edennil ran one unmodified ~5 years (358495, 777104, 948999). Use the file matching your layout (424818). No right-side Alt is fatal (596230); some need right Ctrl swapped with menu and its cap pulled (165234).
- **65% needs a software remap**: left arrow to Alt, Shift and Ctrl swapped (597388, 790484, 139059); on a Ducky, left arrow, Ctrl and Shift, Fn cap pulled (709027, 437491).
- **75%: pop the left-arrow cap for a modifier** — Ctrl, plus a second Alt on down-arrow for the co-op top bar (757274, 189642, 180787), or Shift (586320).
- **TKL was the 2020-05 house preference**, on desk space (988339, 685810). Half-size laptop arrows are unusable (714507, 750528).
- **The 1800/96% split the room** (889922): unworkable, better a $50 dedicated board (131551, 047091); or pull the left-arrow cap, or make it a third modifier under the ring finger on right Alt (187689, 339713); ScaryMouse finds it near his own layout, fine with Ctrl on MO(1) (456286).
- **40% is hard but doable** with a macropad thumb cluster (414528, 749542); the Keyboardio Atreus defeated everyone, wanting three modifiers plus a reachable function key (813492). TheCore uses ~32 keys and few per layer, so a 28-key gamo-28 fits with layering (514098).
- **Avoid the HHKB**, worst layout for this (299389). The Microsoft Natural Ergonomic 4000 curves the wrong way; backspace would need ~40 degrees of tilt (853155, 416514). Razer: entry-level quality, high price (477156).
- **Judge a board by per-finger key budget** (495781): 3-4 thumb (three modifiers plus `/`), 8-9 pinky (`U` floats), 4-5 ring, 4-5 middle, 9-11 index (`0` and `/` float); splits clear it easily (073694). Principles: modifiers on the thumb, no repeated finger in a sequence, high-priority actions on high-priority keys (287604).
- **No SC2 board is sold**; buy any programmable mechanical with 6-7 columns per hand (586782); no purpose-built Core keypad exists (566054). Mell00yell00's 2021 ideal: standard bottom row, four 1.25u modifiers right of a 6.25u spacebar, the 1.25u cap under `/` removed, 1.75u shift with ISO enter for an extra index-reachable key (959046, 050089, 088286), split right shift (262949). ISO enter is fine; `\` just lands better (981492, 357760).
- In passing: Pok3r and Redox (460065, 968916), Roccat Vulcan/Titan (360773), Roccat Isku FX (829150), Steelseries Merc (399446), Leopold FC750R PD with a hardware Ctrl/Capslock toggle (798868), Kira 96% (890816), Niz Plum Micro 82 (615309), Keychron C3 Pro (398782).

## The Fn key is the one thing to check before buying

- **Fn is the only key software cannot remap** — it never reaches the OS (102441, 584576); SharpKeys cannot see it (546826), which sank a Leopold 660m plan (193860). Check where Fn sits (816498) and that it moves (197226); a HK GAMING GK61 couldn't, so DeathDealer switched to an 80% EP-84 Epomaker (726397, 722951).
- **Ducky moves Fn with underside dipswitches**, on the One and One 2 (076184, 555978, 159573, 889898, 916796). Edennil's One 2 Mini table: DIP1/DIP2 both OFF gives the default alt, windows, fn, ctrl, the other three combinations rotating those (558424). On a Ducky Mecha Mini, put Alt on Fn (198826).
- **No dipswitch**: pull the Fn cap (849660), or look for Fn remapping or QMK (973002, 388015); the Aula F75 remaps Fn to right Alt (806738).
- **Pok3r**: dipswitches move Fn; Fn and Pn remap to anything but right control, so swap them (525757, 860097). But **Fn alone does not send Alt** — fine for Alt+key jump-to-location (394334), finicky for camera *centering* (074068, 962940).
- **Beware vendor gaming modes and lighting keys.** A Logitech MX Keys puts an unbindable Fn where Application/Menu was, and its owner gave up on the board; a Razer TKL appears to rewrite Apostrophe to Grave in the file with Gaming Mode on (042241, 652756). Arrogfire's Alt was a lighting key Windows never sees; fix was moving Alt anywhere between space and control (383750).

## Firmware remapping beats software remapping

- **Put the modifier remap in firmware — QMK or VIA.** It lives in the keyboard, survives Windows updates, needs no background process and no cloud, travels to any PC, and matches tournament rules, where pros cannot install software (190652); a Keychron C3 Pro was reconfigured 10 minutes after delivery (398782). Same case in 2020: a dedicated SC2 layer survives alt-tabbing and cannot be flagged (720031, 890851, 514629, 136384). Poletes' best way is a QMK profile or sublayer, Alt on an arrow key, Shift/Ctrl swapped (052610, 733837); firmware beats AutoHotkey for anything permanent (044551). QMK layers also give TheCore in Brood War without AutoHotkey (420168).
- **The LAN argument is the oldest form**: prefer firmware if you play on other machines or at LANs (798868, 871936) — also why Edennil recommended the **Ducky One 2** in 2019-09, its firmware setting repeat delay and rate faster than Windows allows (477156). **Partly contradicted later**: Mell00yell00 sold his One 2 for lacking key remapping (057967), and Bitstorm was unsure whether the feature was firmware or driver (809421); his answer was a Filco Majestouch modded for firmware rewrites, plug and play (586183, 084928).
- **Not programmable? The 1upkeyboards USB-to-USB converter through TMK** (407452). QMK covers what the game won't but is not very accessible (101143).
- **Software routes, in order**: registry (SharpKeys), PowerToys (535167), vendor software, AutoHotkey last. Disabling an AHK script needs a log off and back on (410044), and it must be scoped with `#ifWinActive StarCraft II` so the remap does not follow you out of the game (213623, 659532). **SharpKeys gotcha**: right Ctrl mapped to Alt sends Ctrl+Alt, because it lands on AltGr; map it to Left shift instead (686451, diagnosed 260487).
- Vendor software that worked: the Niz Plum Micro 82 tool (615309), Razer Synapse on a Huntsman (039809, 770089), SteelSeries Apex Pro for macros and modifier rebinds (345695); G Hub and Synapse also named (260989, 717810). Infographics were drawn with keyboard-layout-editor.com (362500).

## Moving the modifiers for TheCore+

- **Goal: all three modifiers adjacent and thumb-reachable with the hand at home** (005446, 601115, 147521). Standing 2024 advice: **move Alt onto the Menu/List key left of right-Control**, no stated disadvantage (541284, 480852), matching 2020's "modifiers in the control/shift/list positions" (839508, 216593). Keys worth stealing: app/menu, right Ctrl, right Alt (482916, 307964).
- **Remap at the key-location level, not in SC2.** Rebinding "Shift+key" to "Menu+key" in-game loses SC2's hard-coded shift behaviour; a firmware/AHK/SharpKeys remap does not, since the game still sees Shift (966224, 186828). Re-check the modifier combinations in the spreadsheet after moving anything (388264).
- **It is optional.** 6g does not assume you can remap modifiers (067085, 852746); the remap is ideal but mostly ergonomic (103207). Keys between Control and Alt are fine too, since Alt only sets base cameras (526678, 283462).
- **Under Core+ the live keys are right-Ctrl, appskey and wherever Alt moved; physical Alt is vestigial** (128370, 577172, 607116). So **do not pull the right shift and right windows caps**: maxisq did, following an old video, and could not deselect units or queue commands, because Core+ shift lives on appskey — the cap he had pulled (063134, 547853, 866567, 480187).
- Worked mappings: Mell00yell00 — Shift = Shift, List = Ctrl, Ctrl = Alt, Alt = `z`, his trash key (721696); jaydik — Fn pulled, list to shift, control to alt, shift to control (959658); Poletes — Left Arrow as Alt, the other arrows unbound after scrolling the screen by accident (459647, 891378).
- **On a compact board, line Ctrl and Shift up and put Alt as close as possible.** JaKaTaK for a Leopold 660m, whose bottom row breaks the Ctrl+Shift camera modifier (706624): shift = ctrl, fn = shift, left of fn = alt (599488, 644057, 587064).
- **Edennil's 5.0 Pok3r setup**: Fn rather than Alt as the modifier, three physical positions kept, every "Alt + key" bind rewritten "Fn + key", plus a layer mapping Fn+key to an arbitrary key (670899, 393812).
- **Too few keys? Ctrl, Shift and Fn as the thumb modifiers, overflow on an Fn layer.** SC2 can't bind an arbitrary modifier+key per command, so `F` and `Fn+F` replace `Alt+F` (917360).
- **NoTruePunk's SharpKeys remap, 2022**, posted in full: right side becomes Ctrl, Shift, menu -> left Alt, Win -> right Alt/AltGr, exploiting SC2 reading AltGr as Ctrl+Alt (198484, 526859, permalink 803247309977944065); he then merged cameras onto the control-group keys (camera = Alt+key, set camera = Ctrl+Alt+key or AltGr+key), freeing `G` and `B` (411081, 430250, 493461). Mell00yell00 warned of conflicts (493396); Edennil liked it as a keyboard-side workaround for Blizzard's hotkey shortcomings (356394) but flagged the Zerg inject fallthrough break (340162); cost is bending the thumb under the palm once a game (350770). See [hotkey-file-editing](/hotkey-file-editing.md).
- **AltGr registers as Ctrl+Alt on Windows** (063623) — what makes that trick work, and what breaks camera binds when AltGr is your only right-hand modifier: a user with Space-AltGr-Win-Fn-Ctrl found AltGr does not register as Alt, and was pointed at Core+ (486130, 614412).
- **Injury case, 2020-11**: Argixx, rock-climbing tendon damage aggravated by the pinky and the thumb-modifier curl, was pointed at v5 or 4.0 plus the Core Plus remap, since v6 puts nearly all control groups on the pinky (457610, 910154); the remap alone fixed it, and he runs regular 5.0 with 6 camera keys (316722, 498133). See [layouts-and-variants](/layouts-and-variants.md).
- **Disable or remove the Windows key** (535682, 513092).

## Split, ergonomic and columnar boards

- **Split ortholinear is the natural home for the layout.** TheCore moves your hand to the wrong side of the board only to get modifiers under the thumb, which a split does natively — why the translation was easy for Edennil (369492, 921680); a split's left half looks like the right side of a normal board at the same spacing (097745). Too few keys on one side means QMK tricks (647987).
- **Edennil's Redox with TheCore 6 on a dedicated SC2 layer is the longest-standing example** (298336, 042984). Its thumb keys sit closer in than an Ergodox's, so he uses `B` for `/` (612988); adaptation is direct key-to-key mapping, `Core 6 J -> Redox A`, `Core 6 ; -> Redox F` (529395). He built a Redox version (761000, 601556), offered to write the process up (829639), and in 2019 offered to help adapt the Core to an Ergodox for someone who had sketched a left-hand mapping (990784, 380745). Assembled from falbatech at ~two months lead time and hundreds over the DIY kit (959731, 849341); he runs a custom layout rather than stock Core (457994) and was waiting on the Elora, a Kyria with a number row (811950). Thumb cluster angle and column stagger differentiate splits (572224).
- **Ergodox, Moonlander, X-Bows, Lily58 and Kyria are all in use** (604874, 713704, 492776, 827529); gcask votes Ergodox (411413). MatosMachine mapped core 6 onto wherever his fingers rest on the ergodox (322836), with QMK camera layers (323034, 516439); someone runs an Ergodox layer for Brood War (331264). In 2020 Ergodox users tended to keep the v5 single home row — MatosMachine rests on `zsdf` and treats slash accessibility as his own problem. Nobody had mapped the Core to a Kinesis (448287); Ergodox and Redox were done by unnamed others (889189); a Preonic went unanswered (018738).
- **x-bows Nature**: andylytical bought one and asked for a 6 mapping (160385); Mell00yell00 warned the right side would be uncomfortable and suggested Core Lite (188000, 716480). andylytical planned core-style keys on the left half with no tilt, Ctrl/Shift/Alt already under the left thumb (276382); four days in he liked it (204224).
- **The ZSA Voyager lacks keys in the right places without layering**: 8 index / 4 middle / 4 ring / 8 pinky / 2 thumb against TheCore's 11 / 4 / 4 / 11 / 3-4 (134036); Skull fell back to v5- or Lite-style modifier camera recall (182745). Edennil documented his own split layout on Posey's board (691040).
- **Posey's split prototype** (715078 on): ergodox-derived, extra column above the thumb cluster, ASDF homing; reached PCB layout, never orderable (949197). Edennil's design feedback (823040, 550019, 644986, 584633): four or more thumb keys in a true arc (Kyria, Elora, Keyboardio as references); an extra, larger far-right column; stronger columnar stagger than the ergodox, since Posey's is 1/8U and 1U is 19.05 mm; index columns aligned to the bottom of `B` rather than the top of `5`; pinky keys at 1.25-1.5U. **Only four thumb keys are critical** — three modifiers and one non-modifier — so let the user pick which four.
- **On a custom board, fit the layout to your hand**, not the hand to the keyboard (906752, 078373, 235752).

## Gamepads, keypads and pedals

- **Gamepads are a bad fit.** MatosMachine would not recommend the Azeron for RTS — 13 finger-accessible keys, slow to hit, better for WASD games (659765, 835679, 569085). On the Nostromo/Orbweaver/Tartarus line, Marc A Wysocki found every game pad he tried junk (265939). Paragon did get an Orbweaver working, Control and Shift on his mouse side buttons, Alt plus extra control groups on the thumb cluster (072906), fitting Edennil's rule: if control, shift and alt fit on the thumb, TheCore fits (763427). A Tartarus runs a modified 6.0 (622151); a Koolertron one-handed keypad went unanswered (665773).
- Foot pedals were a JaKaTaK idea; durability was the concern (627919, 830868).

## Keyboard rotation

- **Rotation is no longer required.** It was expected in v5, where `/` was thumb-only; v6 allows thumb or index (553092, 765697, 456902). Several report little or no tilt (895242, 837669, 026867, 573310), though JuicyJuuce still rotates his (533994). **v6 was designed for a flat board**, index finger lowered instead (681802, 370004, 970857, 647114) — good for laptop users (479116).
- **"Tilt" means rotating the board off parallel with the desk edge, not raising the feet** (842618).
- **Why it helps when it does**: rotating clockwise puts `8` directly above `I`, the ortholinear idea, and moves the modifiers where the thumb can press two at once without wrist strain (344906 with photo; 434792, 434517); go until the columns `8,i,k,,` and `9,[o,l,.]` align naturally (766390). Poletes' first fix for an uncomfortable modifier reach (817193, 868328).
- **Angles over time.** v1-5 wanted 30-45 clockwise (459951, 697237), with Edennil's photo (708841, 456744, 920286). 2020-09: jaydik 10-15 (269665), Poletes ~30 (642316), Mell00yell00 none but set back far enough to rest his forearm (748426). 2021: Mell00yell00 ~10, telling a newcomer at 40 to back off (979520). 2022-23 cluster low — 10-15 (254952, 306344), ~10 (372830), 5-10 (292392), 20 (524998) — with 30 still the standing answer for reach and columnar stagger (746759, 332891, 921791). Throughout: do what feels comfortable (425704). **Edennil from 2020 on: rotating is NOT necessary** (461461); you don't type at that angle, you move your arms over for `glhf`/`gg` (485241).
- **If you cannot reach the modifiers without tilting, slide the keyboard left** rather than angling your wrist; the arm stays perpendicular to the board (126155, 258540). One user reports the no-tilt position feels like a claw mouse grip and adds index-tendon and wrist tension (305920) — unresolved.

## Keycaps: pulling, profiles and landmarks

- **You do not need to remove any keycaps** (758484); The Bee's Knees has none removed, Alt being rarely used (885608, 199411), and the full Core+ swap makes removal unnecessary, all three modifiers ending up adjacent (688533).
- **The caps people do pull are the one next to Alt and the one next to `/`, for thumb room** (717544, 386688). Recipes, with photos: pop the key right of the spacebar so your thumb sits in the hole and reaches Shift instead of going over the tops (032293, 950026); pull the caps between right Alt and right Ctrl so the thumb slides between modifiers (472512); pop the caps between right Alt and right Shift, two photo variants circulating, jaydik and Mell00yell00 both on the "bottom" one (994119, 859868), said to ease `/` and Shift (752562), make injects much easier (397760) and stop accidental shift+control (790762); pull the cap next to Control so the thumb reaches Shift cleanly (545418, 936125). **Dissent: rotate the board instead**, going over the top is easy (415637). Siaal moved Alt to the key left of right Ctrl, fixing his opening-sequence cramp (397898); AvroArrow uses the Fn key there (671063). Hoplite's board has Windows and Fn physically removed, leaving a gap next to Shift; v6 works anyway (629308, 490189).
- **Pulling Fn is now optional — a change of position.** Poletes used to proselytise for it, switched boards, forgot, and has had no problems since (998302); Mell00yell00 still pulls it as a tactile reference, not for reach (947627); the middle option is a lower-profile cap (176290).
- **Landmarks by feel are contested.** For: ReinFórce found rubberized caps immediately helpful when panicking and losing finger position (315469, 052998); JohnKavinski rates shorter or taller caps on control groups highly (130688), though taller caps on the *distant* keys later backfired (576040); Soverange's orientation kit is rubber caps in a pattern, **thumb keys flipped 180 degrees so the thumb does not press the key's edge**, a wrist rest, and ~30 degrees of rotation so `4` and `Q` are level (659549, 558078; will3285 followed it, 369843). Against: rubber caps were slower to recognize than a glance (517004) and felt like they slowed Mell00yell00's fingers (382795). **Most people land on tape** — medical or marking tape, ~$3 at Walgreens, enough friction to find home base (524761, 555178, 897542, 609276) — or on removing the cap, trying it yourself being the only real test (162536).
- **Removing caps to make islands you can find by feel**: right-win and right-alt, `B` and `M` to island `N`, or F6 and F7 to island F5 (126396, 576830, 247869). WASD Keyboards sells multi-colour sets marking the core finger positions (527882).
- **Profile matters more than the board.** The Anne Pro 2 complaint that shift and ctrl are too tall is a keycap profile problem: it ships OEM, DSA caps flatten the difference, and a wrist rest helps as much (390804, 444358, 887690).

## Switches

- **Weight is the only Core-specific variable: 55-62 g is the sweet spot, over 67 g a struggle.** Mell00yell00 runs 55 g silent tactiles and stays under 63.5 g (954206, 331531); bunfoof's MX Blacks make pinky control groups hard (022656). The named pick is the **Boba U4** (silent tactile, 55 g, about MX red weight); up to 67 g is fine for gaming, shorter travel is better, and a tactile bump is technically suboptimal for speed (155944, 247370).
- **Beyond weight, feel is preference** — tactile vs linear has no Core-specific answer (550016) and there was never a consensus recommendation (684618, 720242, for CM Storm Cherry reds and the BlackWidow as a running joke). In use: Edennil's 65 g Zealios in the Redox and Cherry Browns with Green Tea caps in a wood-case Pok3r (638722), marin's Gateron browns (908096), Poletes' Zealios V2, indexing off the gap next to the arrow keys (459647).

## Hand position, thumb technique and the spacebar

- **Float your hand; do not rest your wrist on the desk**, or pressing Shift alone gets hard (528534); the hand rests as if holding a ball (995424), specifically a tennis ball (052692).
- **Thumb technique is disputed and morphology-dependent (2020-09).** bro/"vae": rest the thumb tip on the leftmost part of Shift pointing toward `-` and press by *curling* the thumb rather than pushing down from a rigid rest (299667, 380110). **Edennil disagrees flatly** — curling makes it worse — while allowing there is no perfectly right answer (840445, 674837); Poletes says the wrist looks adducted (172133). **Resolution: hand shape** — bro has a wide palm base with short fingers, Poletes a narrow base with long middle fingers and a short thumb, and each finds the other's position painful (043705, 111443). bro's method also needs board rotation (169030).
- **The spacebar is unused by default — claim it and hit it with your palm.** Bind idle worker, escape or the rally camera there (477871, 927857); rest the palm base below the pinky on it, single-press action only (094514, 876560), since a key that large is very hard to double-tap with palm or pinky (890715). Mell00yell00's palm hits space as a matter of course (689188), which works if your forearm is perpendicular to the board (223218). **Edennil is strongly against it**: it drops the hand far more than it should (342022), and that key then can't be a control group or Esc (643809).
- **The pinky gets stronger with use** — kendo, piano and guitar avoid it no more than this does (432118, 113610); Siaal found the `J` pinky control group intimidating and tiring at first, then a non-issue (514049, 363274). **Downward reaches are the real problem, not fingers**: jaydik finds `M`, comma and period harder than `H`, `B`, `G`, `N` (026755, 314854). On strain, the interossei rather than the forearm tendons should drive key presses (469482).
- **Small hands are a real constraint on 6.0**: Poletes could not comfortably reach three of 6g's keys and reverted to a frankencore (212938). For cancel on `7`, pinky is correct but ring is more reliable under pressure (237746).
- **Wrist pain on 6 usually means tilt, chair or desk height, not the layout.** Solarium quit over sore wrists on 2020-06-02 (067348); TOMES blamed a tilted keyboard (563019); streetplay suggested chair and desk height (066596). Unresolved; he left.
- Where people rest: Mell00yell00 on `J K L ;` with equal time on the row above, base cameras on `0 9 8 7 U` (173249, 985696); vorinvictus, larger hands, on `J I O P`, thumb on Ctrl (369600).

## Binds that depend on physical reach

- **Cancel on `7` is awkward; three fixes** — remove the neighbouring key and put a bumpy artisan cap on `7`, use Space instead, or roll your wrist and hit it with your palm (960754, 606938, 067860, 030356).
- **Ping placement is unsolved.** 6.0's default ping is unreachable; Hoplite floated Ctrl+Space or Ctrl+Space+left-click, and jaydik judged it a reach but too niche to need easy access (226197, 269737, 425468; 230863, 975170, 915034). See [open-questions](/open-questions.md).
- The trash key moves to Backspace (058153) or the Alt right of the spacebar (273281).
- ReinFórce's Terran 6.0 edits: swap `.` and `N`, `N` being hard to reach repeatedly; select-all-army to F8, cycle bases to F9 (702899). Edennil made a similar swap but warns that moving a control group to the middle finger below home row broke other sequences for him (908841). See [layouts-and-variants](/layouts-and-variants.md).

## Mice and mouse buttons

- **Two side buttons are all you need**, and they are the only mouse buttons TheCore binds, so almost any mouse works (262476). Default use is next/previous subgroup, the Core giving you no tab key, and tab reaches the commands of a subset of a mixed control group (196254, 096778, 453439, 110516, 652511, 078500); the pro Core users all have side buttons (525524). Budget pick throughout is the **Logitech G203 at about $20** (706134, 080681, 367435, 876511), with the G305 alongside (951828).
- **What else goes on them**: select-all-warpgates on Shift + back, leaving the gateway control group as a counter (838566, 846657), or warpgate on ctrl+mouse-back (078879); forward and back to cycle production building types sharing one group (384384); extra camera locations (257128) or extra control groups (040714); cameras and awkward pinky control groups on a G502 (418827). Edennil trades previous-subgroup for Select Warpgates (274506). Warp-in on a side button works because your macro2 group holds only robos and stargates, so next-subgroup is enough (469598), at the cost of one subgroup direction (652892).
- **Do not move game commands onto the mouse beyond tab/back-tab.** SwordSmith proposed attack-move and hold-position there (867321) and was rejected: you push against the mouse and fight your own aim (279794, 047390). jaydik prefers easy finger access to all 10 groups over a many-buttoned mouse (582672, 016806); SieStein agrees (029053); Poletes found zero to four mouse buttons a struggle (087070). Luna Cancels considered an MMO mouse under v5, where control group access was worse, and dropped it after v6 (732504); a Razer Naga was floated to avoid strain (842398). Pokebunny can't use thumb buttons at all, squeezing throwing off his mouse hand (217812) — so tab on `Y` was floated, `U` being a camera location (460608).
- **Keep the middle button free for drag scroll.** Holding the wheel to pan is a camera supplement several people use (971670, 780766) and is better for mid-fight scrolling and most scrolling generally (550485). The wheel is not a subgroup control in SC2 (414674); without side buttons, remap the wheel in mouse software or AHK (545088), use middle mouse and lose drag-scroll, or use Luna's alt+shift+p / alt+shift+j (974528, 001062); nw++ tabs through production with ctrl+P (917184).
- **Mouse software caveats**: G Hub won't rebind scroll and its SC2 auto-profile switching failed; older Logitech software and Corsair iCUE per-game profiles work, but iCUE needs its filepath updated after each SC2 patch (011378).

## Key repeat rate

- **Low repeat delay, high repeat rate is what most people go for** (210567, 774766). Delay is how long you hold before it starts re-sending, rate is how often after that (485254). **Go through the registry to get past the end of the Windows slider** (680444). Tradeoff: too low a delay and you queue up a pile of marines by accident (792470).
- **Numbers**: AvroArrow at 150 ms between repeats gets accidental double-presses; Poletes runs 125 ms, fine with practice (675623). Fast repeat alone is a large APM bump, mostly from rapid-firing lings (369794); J1mm1ny measured about +30 APM (325890).

## Non-US physical layouts

- **Set your OS keyboard layout to match the file before diagnosing anything.** Laaxus chased a mystery key that was only wrong because Windows was on BE-FR instead of FR (671059, 842749, 826938, 042394).
- **National keyboards need the matching file**, and the project gives them no real support: Swiss QWERTZ (294258), Croatian on a Danish board (375421), a Scandinavian setup (595716). No Colemak or Dvorak file exists (609888, 119360). thenanox on a Spanish board reads the home row as `JKLÑ` (897172); Moss on Scandinavian plans around `ö`, `å` and `+` (641090).
- **The recurring failure is a key that means something else on your physical board.** A German QWERTZ user saw spore crawler as `^`, the German hotkey file being in use when the intended key is `;`, right of `L` on US QWERTY (921508, 328192, 889930).
