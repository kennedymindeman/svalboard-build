# Layouts and variants

Message ids in parentheses are the last 6 digits of Discord snowflakes in `discord/raw/channel-389438169520799746.jsonl`; a few 6-digit suffixes collide in the 23k-message export, so disambiguate by date if a lookup returns two hits. Agreement counts are floor estimates.

## Which version to run

- **Since 2022-09: v6, specifically JuicyJuuce's updated build, not the file Jak's links point at.** The original v6 shipped without co-op and campaign hotkeys; Juuce's has them and fills in commands left unbound (704050, 068830, 281030). Bose: get Juuce's version, "Jakatak links are a bit outdated" (914489). Asked six-plus times through 2023, answered identically — "If you are still unsure, just get v6" (035594, 589907, 111563, 697493, 618441, 141675; answers 118100, 586815, 814420, 375101, 145770). Edennil called Juuce's file the updated version, not one enthusiast's fork (531971, 079828).
- **Standing exception: already comfortable on an older Core, don't switch — add the missing binds instead.** Edennil: the newest is for people on no version at all (476478, agreed 089469). The gain doesn't repay the relearning (964609, 938867, 857927, 900608, 667364; asked 314196, 487804); standard-to-Core is the real jump, v5-vs-v6 marginal (757087, 456017); a customised v5 isn't worth a switch (460180, 236299); JaKaTaK, same (916678). If v6 doesn't suit you, fall back to v5 and no further (995622); v5 is the recommendation for anyone worried about pinky strain (514813).
- **v5 was never formally retired.** The community stayed a mix (152842); Edennil's 2023-09-20 read is only that most people of recent years are on v6 (523452, 435463, 988043). Newcomers still go to v6 (389234, 089135, 542397); a v4.0 holdover was told to jump straight to 6 unless deeply used to 4 (691038, 003605); a 2022 returning v4.0 user was pointed at v5.0, "4.0 and 5.0 are extremely similar" (459560, answering 620861). Coming back after years away, the basics return fast (099834).
- **Through 2019-11 the advice was the opposite: stay on 5.0, don't chase the betas.** JaKaTaK: relearning each version costs more than it gains next to the gap between standard/grid and any Core — caveated that it was a decent moment to learn 6 on US QWERTY, right hand on the mouse (361556). Superseded within weeks: "any version of 6 is superior to 5. Take the current version of 6 but just don't relearn it when it changes" (2019-11-26, 203371); "IMO 6f is a step above all other versions of TheCore", "Switch now unless you care about coop and camp keys" (2019-12-20, 620224, 098187).
- **2020-01 to 2022: 6 unless you play co-op or campaign, in which case 5.** The capture's most repeated exchange (032401, 123964, 240184, 670667, 825471, 880598, 432907, 270214, 055890, 940574, 879746, 011294, 987688, 091146, 935616, 184396, 950058, 794364, 014026, 464341, 439420, 349248, 329274, 787732) — "v6 unless you have a super good reason not to; if you're new 100% v6" (380353). Nobody in 2020 recommends starting on 5 (818292, 032232, 674124). v5 lost support in 2020-10 (443186); by 2021 its only edge was shipping campaign and co-op, easy to add to 6.0 (799924, 235368).
- **Two days or two weeks into v5, switch now rather than later.** Siaal: v6 felt a sidegrade for a week, then a week back on v5 for co-op showed how much better v6 was (993832). JuicyJuuce and Bose told a 2022 newcomer who "thought 6 was for coop only" to switch after a day of practice (749648, 992754, 809706, 756234); the pinned FAQ's 5-vs-6 answer was stale and predated the Juuce build (281030). See [faq](/faq.md).
- **Don't learn v1–v4.** "V6 is the clear huge break", so old versions are historical (704848, 515709); old downloads are archives (363881, 965771). 3.0 ZRS last updated around 2015 and won't run on the current patch (794729). ShowTime was still on 3.0 PRM US-qwerty with one rebind (149589).
- **Supported set as of 2021-10: Core 6.0, Core 5.0 (and 5.0+), Core-Lite** (490452); nothing else is maintained, and even v5 needs new abilities added by hand (690965). **6.0 is the last version** — Jak moved to his new RTS (357898, 816188). Without QMK, 6G is the best available (345277); the "optimizations" newcomers hear about are already in the released file (834068, 656136).
- **Streamers are not on a public version** — PiG runs a custom layout built for him from core2+ or core3+ (759258).

## Version history

- **v2 through v5 are variations on one paradigm; v6 broke it.** v5 is a customised v2 "Random" layout (724127). Through v5 fingers had strict roles — pinky actions, ring control groups — v6 mixed them (758658, 018324). "5 and 6 share nothing" (930316); the changelog is "every key is different" (547132, 794822, 716161). No written 5-to-6 diff was ever produced (asked 221797, 196762). Upgrading is optional; the ergonomic gains are in every version (878897).
- **The 6 betas, 2019 to 2020-01.** 6b beat Lite for a player needing neither co-op nor campaign, the cost being that fewer people know the binds (042356, 448809, 423233). 6d exists only as a community left-hand conversion (below). **6g released 2020-01-13** (798464) and became the recommendation at once (187914, 336266, 495156, 664287). **6g mainly fixes 6f's missing alternate binds** (944734, 332225, 980412), so check those first when someone on 6f reports a dead key; otherwise the step is minor, nothing to relearn (557324). Siaal: 6 is beta only in being prone to change, not incomplete, and worth swapping to "if for no other reason than the camera hotkeys are on base layer" (571038, 281982, 989740, 795995); JaKaTaK: "6g is baller as fuck and I highly recommend it" (638585).
- Mid-2020 naming is inconsistent: 6g, 6h and 6y all appear as labels with no message defining the scheme.

## What v6 changed, and why

- **Camera locations lost their modifiers.** In 5.0 cameras layer under control groups behind Ctrl+Shift; in 6.0 the first four are bare presses (176528, 797185, 426141, 674982, 273089, 693353, 950810, 525413, 489970, 682817, 211978, 429059, 485491) — the biggest upgrade from v5, the rest mostly a reshuffle. v5 camera 1 was Ctrl+Shift+P; v6 camera 1 is a bare `0` (505282, 757627, 027570). v6 "doesn't use control shift literally at all" (635664) and has no double-modifier binds (367879), superseding older versions that held two modifiers in the start sequence (477632, 055488). For a v5 user: cameras moved to the base layer, tilt is gone, the rest is recognizable (484104, 129738).
- **Why, per Edennil in 2020-02:** v5's camera-jump-on-modifier made injects, tank sieging and rally-cam work fight over the same combinations, so 6.0 re-derived priority across 8 cameras, 10 control groups and command-card actions and put cameras on single presses (271710, 443261, 815008). JaKaTaK: 6 assumes 6-7 control groups, "the cameras are more important than the GVB control groups", the rest "essentially throw aways" (498762, 139212; agreed 901585).
- **The cost is control groups on the pinky.** The last four sit on G, H, B, N (781399, 025365); `B` and `G` are the named casualties, and v5 may suit you if you need them (981251, 578187). Nearly all control groups land on the pinky (457610); v5 is more index-dominant (228575). ScaryMouse's 2021-01 counter: people worried, then got comfortable fast (435112). One convert reverted to v5 in a morning over the thumb action key and mixed camera keys (019882, 870239).
- **Edennil's 2022 statement of the trade:** v5 puts control groups on middle/ring, abilities on pinky/index, cameras layered under; v6 puts abilities on index/ring, control groups on pinky/middle with production on ring, cameras on their own keys above. People tend to prefer v5's control groups and v6's cameras (640424, 462485).
- Secondary: create/add-steal/create-steal on all ten control groups, and a trash key with no modifier (314179, 036282); both backport by hand (965417). **For a game with no camera locations, port v5 rather than v6** (516339, 763081).

## Home positions and keyboard tilt

- **v6 has two resting positions: `JIOP` for macro/economy and `JKL;` for micro and army.** The economy position groups the worker build sequence with the cameras; the army position gives army 1 and 2, a spell and attack-move (944636, 856903, 343929, 978271, 609513, 294629, 689039, 513814, 195846, 907152, 089428, 029536, 003050, 103391, 560716, 518535, 752425). Jak: "Home position is jiokl; shift", depending what you're doing (598722, 837504); the two named in conversation are "wasd" (jko;) and "arc" (jio;). "P is kind of a home key... halfway between a home key and zone 1" (623026). JIOP (tilted) in v3/v5 became JIO; then JKO;.
- **The pinned home-key image marks only `JKL;` in yellow, which confuses newcomers** (294538, 575058; yellow marks home, 069643, 148418). Many rest JIOP anyway, since it puts the cameras one row up (239976); jaydik avoids it because he then types j-p for j-; (481886).
- **Home moved between 5 and 6, and that is what moved attack.** JIOP (index on P) became JIO;/JKL; with the index on semicolon, so attack moved from `P` to `;` and `/` stopped being thumb-only (113182, 594508, 113940, 534028, 839540, 325477, 391590, 187304, 281930, 807714, 152401). The main army control group moved to `J` (546304). Ctrl/Shift/Alt are reshuffled relative to 5 (479119, 552434, 753558). Superseded: the v5 finger map doesn't fully apply to v6 (172618, 612788).
- **v5 was designed for a ~30-degree clockwise tilt; v6 assumes a flat board** (052500, 521892). Siaal: the tilt is why `P` is obvious in v5, and with v6 you don't tilt — confirmed by JaKaTaK, "for version 6, straight" (671300, 980264, 368445, 942411, 635719, 563019, 070275, 781909). v5 home is `J I O P` with thumb on `/`, board rotated 30-45 degrees clockwise (922075, 697237); v6 floats between `J I O P` and `J K L ;`, hits `/` with index or thumb, and rotates less (570624, 212625, 057936).
- **Losing the tilt was a side effect, not a goal.** Jak: it falls out of dual home positions and specific 5-finger positions for dropping spellcasters and injecting — "an effect, not a cause" (866399, 381417, 587947).
- **Most converts keep some tilt anyway.** jaydik settled at ~10 degrees, 10-15 on 6, no wrist issues, and thinks most converts did too (692038, 274197, 154548, 912371); Siaal runs 8 and says tilt "can make a surprising difference" (467146, 651132); TTkB reports up to 45 (144597); TTarps found v6 macro "extremely awkward" flat (173362); thenanox needed 45 degrees to hold JKL; and went back to 5.0 (508372).
- **The v6 spreadsheet says ring finger for `H`. That is a mistake; `H` is pinky** (912988, answering 407454). Finger maps are in the channel pins (563906). **The Team Liquid thread and its finger-placement gif are 5.0 and out of date** (710713, 994110); the race-selection site may lag 6.0 (164924); the jakatak.github.io visualizer is v3-era (200512, answering 256842).

## Cameras and control groups

- **v6 cameras: alt sets, no modifier jumps to the first four, shift reaches another three, ctrl the rally cam** (366800). The base-layer four are `0 9 8 U` (521419, 120734, 537246).
- **Camera 1 is the rally cam** — the only one that doesn't center on the selection when created, so park it at your rally (932756, 962954). Cam 2 main, cam 3 natural (541717); in v6 cam 1 is made with ctrl+`0` (373618).
- **Alt is center-on-selection, hence alt creates cameras** — creating and centering are one gesture (493170, 235934); in 5.0-Plus that is ctrl+alt (619290).
- **Modifier priority, unchanged as of 2023:** Ctrl = add/steal plus ctrl-click select-all; Shift = shift-queue and create control group; Alt = create camera; Ctrl+Shift = create/steal (237850, 563584). **The thumb rests between Shift and Ctrl and never travels** (508427, 821909). In v5 and v6 alike, shift+cg is Create, ctrl+cg is Add/Steal (499304).
- **Only two control-group assignments are prescribed: town hall on `O`, production on `I`** (697778, 014448, 998824). "2 Macro CG" means one CG for Nexus/CC/Hatch and one for Rax/Factory/Starport (336356); one production CG on `I` with tab-through, or three with one on `I`, both read correctly (959865). The numbers beside "Macro 1"/"Macro 2" in the spreadsheet mean nothing (658772). `I` for the main army is not recommended (024170).
- **v6 gives 9 usable control groups: 7 easy, 2 two keys away.** Edennil built this from replay spreadsheets of Lambo, Serral, Reynor, Solar and Dark; only Lambo used more than 7, and 8+ were usually overseers (927076, 954389, 010314). Restated later from top-pro replay packs run through scelite: four accessible cameras matter, control-group use drops off after six (289071, 163348). **Contested:** JimKlide holds "control groups is what core6 is weakest at" (973461); Edennil answers v6 still wins because core5's inject is "absolutely terrible" (567323).
- **The `,` control group (CG 10) is deliberately inverted: press to assign, Ctrl+`,` to recall.** Reported as a German-file bug, ruled working as intended (971813). It is a *trash* group — pulling units out of other groups, de-hotkeying scouts or home tanks — not for recalling an army (646080, 604797), and is commonly misunderstood (866142).
- **Plain "Add to control group" is deliberately unpressable — use Add/Steal** (031552); Steal exists for archon mode (182560). **All-army sits on ctrl+alt+shift on purpose**, parked unpressable rather than spending a good key (035552); people who want it rebind to ctrl+shift+`;`, F6, F8/F9 or a mouse button (497985, 851595, 455115). **Next/previous subgroup live on the side mouse buttons**, replacing Tab (924638, 312199); workable without them but less convenient (637963).
- Suggested Zerg groups on core 6: `O` and `I` macro, `J` and `L` army (167688); hatcheries on `O` (610364); `I` shouldn't be army, since it repeats the finger used by `K` (899521); most units go on the army groups, except infestors and vipers (500244). Mell00yell00 posts a Terran spread (735088). The spreadsheet's second page carries the canonical suggestions, and newcomers are pointed there rather than given a per-race list (912391, 642552, 139712).

## Left, right, and the variants that were retired

- **"Left" and "Right" in a filename mean which hand holds the mouse — not handedness, not a half of the keyboard.** Six years of the commonest newcomer trap (383741, 242017, 085362, 907669, 960214, 360650, 945108, 727135, 710815, 062400, 419264, 128628, 619733, 151098, 041994, 760441, 379724, 975854). The two files are mirrors; the default answer is Right (886601, 612133). Edennil: mouse right -> 6.0 Right, left hand on the keyboard's right side; mouse left -> 6.0 Left, right hand on the left side; Core-lite = mouse right, left hand on the left side (520701, endorsed 944805). Don't use the "left" file with a right-hand mouse — use Core Lite (313376). Left-handed binds are in the Google Doc but nobody in the 2020 channel had used them (193007).
- **Left-handed 6d existed only as a community conversion.** Trasher mirrored 6d against the spreadsheet's "Control Groups Cameras" tab; known gaps are the missing mirror for BackSlash (Hallucinate Stalker, Turn Camera Left, Spray) and the menu/UI/chat keys (448596).
- **Race, hand-size and grid variants are gone, removed on purpose.** Edennil: "we did away with those… just confusion with way too many versions" — z, t, p, r crossed with s, m, l gave 12 versions per hand (045377, 033694, 296850, 321364, 763468). Versions 1-3 shipped 24 files (4 race x 3 hand-size x 2 keyboard hand), 4-5 shipped 4 (left/right x Core/Core+), 6 ships 2 (892531, 730217, 955817, 968370, 244210, 626134, 246130). v3.0 was the last with the splits; v4.0, Nov 2017, ended the RZS ("right, zerg, small") naming (766390, 947732, 060467, 474556, 941886, 615644, 989436). What remains is "Random Race + Medium Hand Size + Regular Core" (070238; race files confirmed gone 301129); the splits were a barrier to entry and the gain of one over another tiny next to switching at all. Old guides, videos and the web demo still advertise them (895626, 873222, 898862, 059996).
- **Small hands: move the hand, rotate the board, or rebuild the variant yourself.** "it ok to move your hand, let it flow", remap a nearer key to Ctrl, or hand-convert toward the old small layout (715589, 134430, 278209); later, shift every finger key one right and re-home whatever falls off the edge (880642). Stretched index: curl it more, or rotate (965748). **Small hands argue for the Core, not against it** — Poletes has 16 cm hands, reaches everything on the Core and could not on standard (390815, 960848, 239338).
- **Large hands: shift the layout left**, "a solid hand size customization" (010113, 173972). Dropped in v4 but rebuilds cleanly with a search-and-replace moving every bind one key left (761640, 961951, 501208); see [hotkey-file-editing](/hotkey-file-editing.md).
- **What TheCore fundamentally is:** your hand moves to the non-standard side of the keyboard so the thumb can press all three modifiers, as split-ergo boards have done for years (509204, 571093, 384687, 530911); if you don't want to move your hand there, don't use TheCore (485864). **You do not need to remove keycaps** (571093), and hitting the keys between Control and Alt is not a real problem (694770); if it is, apply the Core+ modifier move (324417).

## TheCore+

- **In 6.0 "Plus" is not a different file — it is an optional physical modifier remap done outside the game.** 6g and 6g Plus are the same file: rebind the modifiers, change no hotkeys (230708, 015746, 103207, 914560, 991744, 549470, 165049, 587890, 838840, 267850, 653052). Edennil: earlier versions shipped separate regular and plus files that changed some control-group and camera modifiers, "but it wasn't really needed and we removed the additional file" (310914). No Core+ file exists to download (740446, 473866, 045632, 108998). **Superseded: in 5.0 and earlier Core+ was its own download with different binds** (985146, 850635), 5.0 having used a lot of ctrl+alt (590293). The remap uses **external** software, not in-game settings (775795); SharpKeys works (917544).
- **The remap**: physical shift sends ctrl, physical ctrl sends alt, physical alt sends shift (056734, 809877, 758888, 522842, 735121); stated elsewhere as ctrl to shift, alt to ctrl, shift to the win-list/menu key (529637, 737027, 801040, 062870, 448066). It exists because SC2 will not let you rebind shift away from queue-actions (415872).
- **What it buys, by era.** In 5.0+ the remap enabled actions otherwise impossible, "like adding to control group without stealing"; Poletes, 2020-05: on the current version all 6g+ does is make it more ergonomic, since the rest position is shift but SC2 uses ctrl far more (843777, 880725). It puts three modifiers adjacent under the thumb where regular Core has two adjacent and a third elsewhere, forcing a few camera and control-group differences; abilities identical (133160, 425734). JuicyJuuce: on a normal keyboard the thumb alone can't hit alt+control or alt+shift (174668). Core+ adds Create/Steal control group and camera slots 5-8; 6.0 already has that Create/Steal, leaving Create Cam 5-8 on Ctrl+Alt+`0,9,8,J` (987772). With cameras on the base layer in v6 nothing is left to improve (119360, 261441, 672848); the distinction "was more important in previous versions" (267170), and Plus mainly puts Ctrl on the biggest physical key (123080). **Skipping it breaks nothing** (806593, 512604, 119902, 861488, 976266).
- **Half-measures are fine.** Leave shift and ctrl standard, move only alt, and "you're at least 99% of the way there" (806387, 691836); "Half plus" moves only Alt to the win-list key (801040, 062870, 448066).
- **Don't bind shift to alt when building your own Core+.** JaKaTaK: shift is a home key and alt the least used of the three (245378); Panda argues the opposite (071620). Core+ modifier priority is Control, then Shift, then Alt, positions picked by what Jak and others found easiest — relocate if your hand disagrees (064160, answering 979403, who found Alt right of Shift odd given Shift carries more functions). ScaryMouse dissents entirely: with his board and hand size his thumb already rests on ctrl (335098).
- **Core+ assumes a keyboard with a large left shift.** Bose's board had a short shift and long enter, and Core+ "didn't make as much sense at all" until he changed keyboards; JuicyJuuce agreed the FAQ needs a revamp (800986, 463986). Still undocumented as of 2023-02. For UK vs US there is "no actual difference" in version 6 Core+ (134043).

## TheCore Lite

- **Lite is the answer whenever the hardware or the hand can't do the thumb-modifier reach: laptops, boards with an unusable right side, and anyone unwilling to leave the left side.** Everything concentrates around QWASZX with modifiers on the pinky (958651, 862421, 016795, 319391, 473225, 739008, 716480, 700928, 863794, 703552, 254142, 043134, 068042, 205876, 143707, 504021, 937579). Regular Core on a laptop makes your arm cross your torso (962947). Lite reaches only to H (945408) and shares control-group and camera keys behind modifiers where regular Core gives them separate keys (046548).
- **Lite and Left are different things.** Lite keeps everything on the left side; Left is for people who mouse left-handed (508969, 855946, 702155, 848233). Lite has no left-handed version (702896) and there is no "CoreLite Plus" (082792). **Whether Core Lite is the left-handed version was asked in 2020-05 and never answered** (806017, 468021); see [open-questions](/open-questions.md).
- **Core and CoreLite are different layouts by different authors, sharing only the goal.** Common: minimize reach, minimize repeating a finger, consider full action sequences. Different: Lite keeps the hand in the standard left-hand position for familiarity, Core mimics a split-ergo board so the thumb hits the modifiers (835658, 668343, 181078, 654504). Core is the more ergonomics-focused; Lite is equally efficient (493520). "Both are far better than standard" (727888).
- **Lite is the easiest thing in the family to learn.** Mell00yell00: standard to corelite is "borderline enjoyable", "super easy to learn and retain"; core6 is more intuitive but Lite wins on familiarity (004714, 226251, 373235). Altafen runs it on a laptop (531294); Mell00yell00 went Corelite -> 6 and never used 5 (450149). Its command card shows letters rather than symbols, which helps for the first week (016665, 952007). Its Ctrl / Ctrl+Shift semantics come from TheCore 3, where Shift was jump-to-camera (021417, 961916, 548447). Known flaw: Halt and Lift share a key (134528, 993133). Different hand position, so not a drop-in for someone trained on Core (927060).
- **Lite is a separate project with its own repo and better docs** — github.com/bobo38/TheCoreLite, its own channel pins and a cheat-sheet PDF, not the main spreadsheet (018664, 386463, 688208, 675806, 792016, 271472, 527306). Less used and less supported (949289) but still updated; its channel is "kinda dead" (711355). See [links](/links.md).

## Regional keyboard layouts

- **Match the file to your physical keyboard, and pick qwerty / dvorak / colemak by your top-left letter row**: QWERTY, `',.PYF`, or `QWFPGJ`; filenames look like `TheCore6g_right_US_qwerty.SC2Hotkeys` (018984, 777576). basedharry's control groups came out `n y u j` because he took the Colemak file on a UK keyboard; the fix is the UK QWERTY file (232980). Use the UK qwerty file if your board has AltGr, even in the US (329918, 923816).
- **v6 shipped officially in US QWERTY, right-handed, only**, stated repeatedly from 2020-01 to 2020-04 (834906, 456451, 750933, 765127, 693707, 272160, 284968, 519105); others were promised when it left beta, and meanwhile someone would run the converter for you, output untested. **v5 by contrast shipped in several national layouts, including QWERTZ** (927648, 388240, 210634); SPARROW kept the AZERTY files current (235010); Siaal kept hand-converted 6 files and would DM them (563098).
- **Community-converted national files exist but are unreliable.** As of 2020-06 the Drive folder carried community conversions — a right-handed Swiss one turned up (923403) — and a converter lives on the GitHub (806146). Localized files exist for Hungarian, Swiss, German and Scandinavian keyboards (201199, 621170, 538088). **Fix errors by hand against the qwerty layout** (312459). Failures: a Swedish player's file didn't match his board (940467); Poletes said 6h wouldn't work for the Swiss and Scandinavian special characters and that he can't code (634266, 454244); the ABNT2 (Brazilian) file gave a K-K spawning pool, fixed with the build-basic alternate (460413, 292531).
- **Brazilian ABNT2, resolved by 2023-11: use the US QWERTY file, not the Portugal file** (245349, 957885, 638472; earlier 415783, 671096, 893626). **Swiss German: the right modifier is Alt Gr — rebind it to Left Alt** (190110).
- **AZERTY genuinely changes things** because of its odd keys (647033, correcting 862270, who said hand position is all that matters); AZERTY files for 5 and 6 exist "somewhere floating around on this discord" (414696). **QWERTZ has no file — customize it yourself** (231028, 341501). German users note the unused "Ä" key right of the home row as free real estate (661607), with no official use for it. **No Colemak file exists** (asked 609888).
- Non-US users who won't hand-edit stay on v5 or remap their OS to US. Old and national files sit in Jak's Drive under "3. Download thecore" then "hotkey files..." then "old versions" (324372; the `OLD VERSIONS` folder, 795231). See [tools-and-scripts](/tools-and-scripts.md) for the converter, and [keyboards-and-hardware](/keyboards-and-hardware.md): a 60% board is fine, since the F1–F12 binds are menu functions only (090708, 725897), while laptops with half-height arrow keys break the layout (714507, 750528).

## Campaign and co-op coverage

- **Since 2023, "Core6 is multiplayer only" is superseded: Juuce's build binds campaign and co-op** (083910, 748729, 558016, 433208). v6 covers both, but ladder was the priority (487558, 208900).
- **From 2019 to 2022 the 6 line bound multiplayer keys only** — no co-op, no campaign, no arcade, the stated reason to stay on 5 or Lite for those modes, and the most repeated caveat in the 2020 chunks (171743, 830144, 195286, 847616, 132673, 986507, 741708, 355020, 814110, 704768, 121748, 544683). Hence odd-looking campaign binds — Diamondback on `D` is "just the sc2 default key" (928553).
- **"Multiplayer only" excludes campaign and co-op, not team games and not 1v1** — a recurring newcomer trap (019059, 047336, 234024, 143071, 636305, 763132).
- **Why co-op specifically broke v6.** Stukov's infested banshee has burrow and cloak on the same key, so the file errors (187584, 333221). The only fix is to move burrow or cloak off every cloaking or burrowing unit, and moving cloak makes the ghost bindings awkward (257354, 518538) — or sacrifice the banshee and ghost (917717, 561392). Workaround: mark the file read-only and live with the error (608896). Through 2022 the recommendation was **run v5 for co-op** (038972, 643988).
- **Remapping co-op keys in-game corrupts your versus binds.** Hoplite did that and was told to throw the file away and redownload a clean 6.0 (256707, 690021, 177866) — see [hotkey-file-editing](/hotkey-file-editing.md).
- **Rolling your own, pre-Juuce.** Four answers: use 5 for single player (102952); use grid for campaign, since even v5 doesn't key most hero commands (137812); "download 6 and add in the coop buttons as you go" (450438); or do it yourself, as Mell00yell00 did for Zerg, Terran and Nova campaign keys in about 25 minutes (271370, 235932, 549374). For v6 you bind campaign units yourself — mostly variants of existing units plus hero units, the top bar already bound — in its own save file so it cannot break multiplayer (947659, 870888, 739221). No public zerg campaign file was known in 2020. Co-op needs less work: build hotkeys are largely right, hero abilities and custom units are what's missing (378516). Nobody shipped an official one: the 6.0 builders had played zero campaigns and the parser that produces the variants is broken (099840, 632640). The Nova campaign may work on v5, not v6 (491732). A community campaign profile is at permalink 871778299037233215 (246524). The site's "other games" section is community contributions with no research behind it (374216). Sora's practice alternative is campaign or vs Elite AI until you beat all three races (902630).

## Race-specific placement and per-race advice

- **Race files were dropped for one "random" layout**, so the camera modifiers must suit Zerg injects for everyone (636324, 033960).
- **v6 is a Zerg-driven design, and Zerg is where switching pays.** jaydik: "core 6 does feel a bit zerg centric (it was the motivation for a lot of the changes)" (466892), and he would switch for the injects alone (970791, 901002). Poletes: the gain is far larger for zerg (935893, 323890); his 2020-08-07 split is **zerg yes** — better on injects and larva trashing — and **protoss and terran no**, neither better nor worse, either way "a complete mechanics reset" (025981, 302366, 715227, 259581, 360115). ScaryMouse: for zerg the cameras alone justify it; elsewhere nice but not decisive (071233, 856650). Edennil, 2022: v6 "opens up inject options that 5.0 doesn't have", since v5's layered cameras constrain the inject method — which matters for zerg, not terran or protoss (919882, 496712). Mell00yell00: v6 works for all races but is "probably most optimized for Zerg" (010428).
- **The Zerg inject sequence in v6: hit `/` with the thumb.** Edennil (797618): `i` (ring, select queen), then `0` (index) `/` click, `9` (middle), `8` (ring), `U` (pinky), each `/` click; inject and camera order is interchangeable (052684). jaydik's mnemonic: `i` injects, `o` hatches, `p` drones, then `J` army / `K` ability / `L` micro / `;` attack (048169).
- **Zerg larva: press `O` then hold `P`, not O-P-P.** Hatcheries put select-larva on as many keys as possible for this (789364, 116891); where holding fails, use a different finger for select-larva than for the unit (976923). Nearly every zerg production key carries select-larva too, except infestor, viper and ultralisk (021629).
- **Zerg ability placement follows priority, not mnemonics**: all morphs go on `P` and outrank spells there; `P` is also the preferred rapid-fire key, so bile lives there; fungal isn't rapid-fire, so it goes on `K`, the go-to non-rapid-fire key (446791, 431390, 606047). Where two want one key — morph viper vs morph ultralisk — "there just isn't room, so they were done by priority" (762188). **Burrow is on `[` and `]` for normal Zerg units; `K` is burrow only on lurkers**, where it is siege up/down (598110, 118472, 281932).
- **Disruptor belongs on `[`, not `k`;** the spreadsheet contradicts itself with two robo facility rows (312074, 693161).
- **Race-specific customization is endorsed where the default hurts one race.** Edennil on Terran wanting jump-to-location on the shift layer: "This is definitely territory for race specific customization" (300330); JaKaTaK suggests select-all-warpgates on a side mouse button for Protoss (473354). jaydik offraces without changing layout (983913); if an earlier version suits you, don't switch just to be current (169607).

## Rationale for specific keys

- **`I` is production structures and `K` is a unit-ability key, and they must not collide.** Both are ring-finger keys, so production structures carry no `K` bind (200896, 916864). `I` was chosen because those facilities need no ring finger for their own abilities (507132, 948674, 411719). **`O` is Nexus/CC/Hatchery because probes sit on `P`** (929759, 577070). v6 *requires* `i` = military production because of how it freed the cameras: v5 kept cameras under modifiers with strict finger separation; v6 raised four to no modifier so the Zerg inject works, paying by letting the ring finger carry both control groups and abilities (138206, 135991).
- **Every version has two build-basic keys; use the one on a different finger from the structure.** In 6.0 they are `J` (pinky) and `;` (index): pinky structure, start with `;`; index structure, start with `J` (685930, 521489). SC2 shows the alternate only on hover (825179). **Build Probe / Build SCV is `P`, not `K`** — the 6.0 spreadsheet's per-race sections disagree with its top section, and the top section is right (087484, 019512). **Worker is not on `-` in 6.0**, contradicting 5.0 muscle memory (326220, 833263).
- **`/` is dual-use in 6.0: index by default, thumb when the index would otherwise fire twice in a row** (119936, confirming 478272). Zerg inject sits there as a thumb press on US/UK qwerty, as do many build-advanced sequences (167456, 280479) — `/` thumb for build advanced, `/` index for spellcasters, to prevent finger repetition (145385, 485328, 754395, 002887, 911882, 184660). **Superseded:** versions 1-5 dictated thumb for `/` and told you to rotate the board for it (836352, 346122). Mell00yell00 dissents and thumbs everything (304256, 429746). All modifiers are thumb (600306, 913365).
- **"You should never use finger repetition in the core"** — Edennil, on someone pressing `/` then `;` with the same finger; needing to repeat a finger usually means you are missing an alternate keybind (686336).
- **`K` is the one precision key; everything else is rapid fire** (455450, 836590).
- **Spacebar is unbound by default; put single-press actions there** — select all army, idle worker, trash control group, stop — but nothing needing speed inside a sequence (385242, 112431). Mell00yell00 uses it for idle worker, hit with the palm (313237).
- Bose's summary: `K` and `P` on alternate fingers cover the gist of every command a unit has, with camera and unit hotkeys close together (302116).

## Customising

- **Tweaking the official file is encouraged**: "Oh hell yea man! We encourage tweaking" (121428); "self bind for now" when a bind is missing (490014).
- **But not before you can play the stock layout.** Bose to a newcomer redesigning camera keys on day two: learn Core 6 first, since most of the logic was long planned and shaped by the community (592192, 289098). See [learning-and-practice](/learning-and-practice.md).
- **If v5's cameras bother you, move to v6 rather than patching v5.** "V6 was designed around fixing the cameras" (568415); TOMES: "worth relearning some stuff to get the better experience" (453954). Same answer later to a v5 user unhappy with ctrl+shift cameras: "Your options are core 5+ or core 6" (853511, re 560774).
