# Open questions

Message ids in parentheses are the last 6 digits of Discord snowflakes in `discord/raw/channel-389438169520799746.jsonl`; a few 6-digit suffixes collide in the 23k-message export, so disambiguate by date if a lookup returns two hits. Agreement counts are floor estimates.

## v5 versus v6, and the pinky load

- **Never resolved. The 2024 advice is only: don't switch if you're already fluent (476478, 995622).**
- Pinky load is the complaint. 2019: "heaaavy on the pinky" (198879) against "But it's good" (545423) and JaKaTaK's 6-7 control group assumption (404565). 2020-04, JimKlide went back to v5 after pinky and ring strain from double-tapping to recall groups, longer camera travel and heavier `/` — v5 avoided pinky-held modifiers, "but I find that double tapping puts even more strain" (463318, 648464). Six control groups sit on the v6 pinky (ScaryMouse 228695), enough that Sora won't switch (839562).
- JaKaTaK's rebuttal, 2020-06, with a call to get aligned (427065): v5 gave the pinky 6 functions and v6 gives it 8, but v6's extras (patrol, the last four groups) are rare, so v5 probably works it harder, "at best they're even" (845683). jaydik and Poletes agree (026755, 649582); SieStein (227313, 274752, 528903) and JimKlide, "Pinky is way too overloaded" (888873), don't. streetplay: Zerg hits hatch, inject, creep, army and scout fast, so why is the fifth on the pinky (180901, 896370)?
- 2020-10, Pokebunny: the index is strongest but confined while the pinky roams `U H J N M` (802048, 771158); JimKlide had two weeks of pinky aches, since gone, and still can't find `N` or `G` (358164, 224193). Against: v6 drops the pinky as a modifier, so it does less than standard or grid (986324), and "N/M isn't that ambiguous" with practice (931882). Left open (893662); restated 2021 as disqualifying for an injured hand (457610) versus everyone adapts fast (435112).
- For v6: inject "is like 50% of the reason we made 6" (208189), a clear win to jaydik and TOMES (507274, 014886), "significantly better" for zerg (302366). Against, from JimKlide: if camera hotkeys never troubled you 6 isn't necessarily better (772362), and core6 is weakest where zerg needs it (973461) — met by Edennil and jaydik's unreserved yes (567323), both sides agreeing v5 is fine if firmware solves the inject (851822). Unadopted zerg fix: swap the trash group for a real control group, moving one onto the ring finger (185052, 727306).
- The only outside evidence anyone brought is gcask's 1986 tapping study: thumb, index and middle tap fastest, but index and little are safest in error terms — "Pinky great for lighting 'reflex' actions, middle/index great for mindless tapping" (477292, 135809).

## How many control groups, and how they split

- **Never settled. One production group plus tab, and three or four separate groups, are both still defended in 2024 as correct readings of the design (959865).**
- JaKaTaK's rent model, 2020-06: functions are tenants, "The home row is where the prime real estate is", and attack-move and the town hall group pay that rent where the 10th control group, 8th camera, idle worker, patrol and build ultralisk can't (845683) — so 2-group macro beats 4-group, real estate beating easier rally (039040). Poletes: playstyle dictates the count, ShoWTimE and Stats routinely use 8 and reach 9-10 in long games, PartinG runs 3 unit groups, so "it is an absolute must to have at least 8 control groups" (017728), with his ShoWTimE control-group map as the argument's one concrete artifact (178400). JaKaTaK discounts redundant per-Nexus binds — "I almost never see 8 actual CGs (non-redundant)" — and suspects a sample-size problem (907677, 922332), half-conceded, since Poletes mostly watches those two (756585, 324847).
- Terran production, 2020: one group plus tab or three (SieStein 180998)? TOMES and ScaryMouse recommend one plus tab on the m4/m5 mouse buttons, easier rally tracking and freed keys (302023, 734292, 054258); SieStein found tabbing uncomfortable, which fed his return to v5 (939675, 528903). Pokebunny needs CC, rax, fac and port separate for rallies, especially hellion-banshee, settling provisionally on `I O H N` (564621, 611904, 053628). Siaal was forced onto one production key by v6 and finds it fine, tab and shift-tab both bound (726337); JimKlide as Zerg keeps all tech in one group and clicks the building in the panel (029120). Colston asked for a 4-macro Terran split and Mell00yell00 declined — "I dont have any recomendations on what would be a good 4 macro setup" — describing his own 8-group scheme instead (569499, 114311).
- Army groups: JohnKavinski keeps four, uses two, can't yet execute four locations on TheCore (692820, 597353); Byun uses many, "But yeah it's not needed to play at the top level" (069255), and Harstem too, though probably on default (957598).
- One unit in several groups: don't, especially as a beginner (691541); Hann relies on it (999764). Should Shift steal? yjzhou wanted create-and-steal, seeing no use for overlapping groups as terran (006336), and got the baneling and tank cases plus "you can change whatever you like in the core if you don't use something" (229738); Mell00yell00 was separately trialing dropping create entirely (016020).
- "The tap" — single-tapping a production group to check production — got worse in 6. Raised by gcask (328203), defined by Edennil as e.g. Terran tapping the CC group for SCV production (795906); JaKaTaK conceded "The tap was an oversight" (792533) and floated pinky-middle over pinky-ring (502155), against gcask's view that both macro groups belong on one finger (696012). Unsettled.
- Layering control groups onto ability keys (Ctrl+QWERASDZX create, Alt+QWERASDZX select) drew Edennil's 2023 critique: select-control-group behind a modifier is slower for something urgent and frequent, and Shift+key for add-and-steal costs shift-queueing on that key (641258, 500867). The asker was unpersuaded (107153).
- Edennil's own open question, 2024-07-14: is three fingers on control groups, one on ability keys and the thumb on both modifiers and one ability key viable? Core has used two fingers for groups and two for abilities, which forced camera locations under modifiers and constrained Zerg injects; v6 fixed the camera problem but "still feels a bit short in the number of easily accessible control group keys". His own objection: two ability fingers are needed for sequences like Stim then Attack, and Stim on the thumb would break shift-queueing it (172618, 240702). No replies.

## Zerg injects

- **Standing recommendation since at least 2023: control-group the queens, use camera location hotkeys, don't press shift, hit camera and inject (thumb) at essentially the same time (960215, 331494, 983188), full sequence at 057075.** Pros dropped shift around 2017-2018 and Core followed (752400, 244186).
- Shift injects were argued throughout and never conceded. Against: queens wander (433882); "shift injects is bad, don't do that" (076882); don't hold shift to queue injects across bases (Siaal 829590, Poletes 068616, jaydik 492510, 063377, 451350). For: shift is fastest late-game (010749); braindead shift injects are bad but done slowly it's the same with fewer keys (811028); jaydik runs the camera cycle twice instead (609640); Edennil's accommodation is cameras 1-4 on shift (784000). The 2020 v5 round: a since-deleted user holds Ctrl+Shift and alternates base cameras and clicks; Poletes says that queues injects, is slower, inconsistent and always produces wandering queens (490942, 938281), against the count that with all queens above energy the actions are the same (118826). Altafen fact-checked the appeal to Lambo's video and found no mention of shift (319968); Poletes narrowed to a queen with 2x inject energy injecting the wrong hatch (852545); the two turned out to define "wandering queen" differently (696155).
- Hotkey the queens or box them, open since 2020: hotkeyed is faster, boxing survives a queen dying (Siaal 360582, 918666). For: Poletes, "it is required to hotkey queens" (575356, 517544, 318917, 294922); jaydik's "I 100% suggest", corrected to "66% faster I guess, not 100%" (049799, 960162); streetplay's arithmetic, 4 seconds to 1 on a 30-second cycle is 10% of the cycle (185985, 618236), with a Scarlett clip (977809). Against: Mutaller, "popular but is not good", "I inject 4 bases in 4 seconds with boxing" (446318, 144745, 028172), later conceding "saying it is not good is wrong" (063460). Siaal's undisputed context: "the core was made as a design decision to not hotkey inject queens", and hotkeyed queens are painful to recreate and to manage when one becomes a creep queen (104393). Whether to group them at all splits the same way — Poletes "you are supposed to" (141224) and jaydik's favourite thing about v6 (616530), against J1mm1ny dropping the group to free `I` (892117), TOMES never learning it (967188), Siaal grouping creep queens on `I` only (255648). Shared complaint: a sniped queen desyncs the sequence (Siaal 797578; jaydik 031700, "no great solution to it"), mitigated by a spare creep queen to peel off (875530).
- Base camera versus saved camera locations, argued 2020, 2022 and 2023 with no verdict. jaydik: "I'm pretty anti-base cam" (417034). Poletes: it cycles rather than jumping to a specific base and includes macro hatches (339677), and "the cams were literally designed around injects" (739827). Edennil: identical speed at four cameras, base cam wins on simplicity, you lose at bases five through seven, and pros never actually jump seven hatcheries (724476, 961600, 687012, 031242). Hammershaft and later Fix argue base cam (822481, 656081). Edennil's v6 sequence: select queen CG, `0` index, `/` thumb, left-click hatch, `9` middle, `/`, click, `8` ring, `/`, click, `U` pinky, `/`, click, with base cams centred on the hatchery so the mouse stays mid-screen (031578, 615051). The 2024-onward variant, whether cycle-town-halls is usable for injecting, ends the same: inconsistent behaviour reported (855371, 603521), maybe it remembers the last base cycled to (281771), and Edennil points back at camera location hotkeys (289360).
- How many hatcheries: "never really recommended to inject more than 4 hatches, core or otherwise", many do 3 (Siaal 747860, 859166); jaydik injects 1 to 4 (247263, 541001) and elsewhere only three to save time, drawing "well thats wrong" (408588, 909544). Reasons offered: past about 6 bases units are more larva-efficient and queens get pulled for transfuse (566741, 236116), and larva is rarely the constraint, gas is (044210, 357644). Triggered by "why not all of them" (069333), and returned unresolved in 2022 (480754 versus 092350).
- Inject order `0-9-8-U` versus `U-8-9-0`: no consensus (252416, 700787). zeky_85's rapid-fire rotation (inject plus backspace) broke once injects became queueable, a queen taking two commands for one hatchery and then wandering (215938; Sora 109302); his three-key workaround was reported working but untested (800134).

## Larva, eggs and the trash group

- **Add-and-steal versus create-and-steal on the trash key was never decided. JaKaTaK declined to pick: "Either is fine, add is more recycling bin, create is more trash" (995476, 919977).** In 2020-05 Poletes and jaydik both said create+steal and changed their own files; JaKaTaK said add was deliberate so you never lose a unit, then agreed create has more utility (987713, 725806, 154812). The shipped file never changed.
- Whether larva trashing is worth doing is still split: for (730182, 202507), against as actions spent for little gain (186443, 558632), with JaKaTaK himself unhappy with it in the add-to-`L`-control-group case (351034). Edennil ctrl+clicks eggs and calls trash "an extra point of failure" (496286) while JuicyJuuce switched the other way (038387); the later round is JuicyJuuce for (544358) against Edennil (943344) and Fix (585354).
- Keyboard or command card for larva: jaydik's `O ; ,` keeps the mouse put (896370) at the cost of tracking spent larva; JimKlide says clicking the card is pure muscle memory (644162, 610497).
- Give up the trash recall to reclaim a ninth real control group? JimKlide did (591046); jaydik says a zerg who larva-trashes can't afford to (094507).
- JaKaTaK relayed Ninjury's proposal to add and steal eggs and larva into one group, then either trash it immediately or use leftover larva as an overlord trigger, arguing the immediate option is more reliable (349696, 011083). Open.

## Camera locations

- **Edennil's own modification — cameras 1-4 bare, 5-8 on Control, deprioritising the rally camera — is recommended often enough to be near-standard advice but is not in the shipped file (782161, 549076, 510314, 279410).** He likes Mell00yell00's F5 variant but won't put F-keys in the official file, F-row positions varying by board (324318).
- The shipped v6 camera binds are disputed by their own co-developer, who says the execution fell short (867996, 716561) and published a replacement (060718); the shipped Juuce file keeps the original deliberately, to stay aligned with the official layout (279188). Newcomers keep rediscovering the mismatch between `Alt + P` to create and `Shift + 9` to jump (392796, 226141).
- Shift on camera keys at all: Mell00yell00 strips Shift from every camera create and jump, because holding Shift to queue commands makes him hit the wrong camera (095464, 700352). Edennil has never made that misclick and keeps Shift, but concedes the logic and asks whether Mell00 also drops Shift+control-group; he doesn't, and said he'd watch for it (690122, 681342).
- More modifier-free cameras: wanted by Alpako and Kidze (179112, 915740). Edennil's brainstorm, explicitly ideas not a recommendation (365426, 220072): cams 1-4 on `7`-`0` and 5-8 on F5-F8, all modifier-free at the cost of reaching; or `6`-`0` for five or six modifier-free cameras, moving Cancel and Idle Worker to spacebar and the vacated `U`. Left open.
- Protoss cameras on Shift or Ctrl: four people built variants (MatosMachine 811833, Harri 030195, Poletes 591080, Siaal 448853) and nobody landed it. Poletes's structural objection is that warp-ins and injects want opposite camera setups (406128); on MatosMachine's second draft, "slightly better... actually probably equivalent" (870804).
- Numbering direction: jaydik goes `0` main, `9` nat, descending, the flip from ascending being a mirror rather than a shift (411274); J1mm1ny always went 1 to 0 (920499); AvroArrow backs descending because main and natural land on index and middle (837578).
- Ordinal or geographic: AvroArrow argues geography, so you never rebind mid-game (644894, 700837); jaydik, Mell00yell00 and Sora keep 8 as "my third" wherever it is (841670, 839530, 041266), with "I dont think it actually matters" (034804).
- Asked, never answered: why cams 5, 6 and 7 aren't `Shift+I` / `Shift+O` / `Shift+P` left to right (652507); why camera 5 skips `Shift+0` (812949, 540500); why `Ctrl+9` (655381); whether the v6 spreadsheet or the file is authoritative on camera 8 and the camera 5-7 modifiers (186418, 278170).

## Modifiers

- **Which modifier goes where has never been ruled on; every round ends in personal preference.** JaKaTaK's one hard constraint is keeping control and add/steal on the same key (919977); incompatible TheCore+ mappings were already circulating by 2020-01 (945896, 122304, 891264, Siaal's at 480855, RoboZerg's at 318248).
- JaKaTaK says shift must not go on alt, shift being a home key and alt the least-used modifier (245378); Panda argues the opposite at length (071620). Mell00yell00 thinks physical shift as ctrl and ctrl as shift "is probably optimal" but won't relearn it (864834); JimKlide kept the original for muscle memory, and because you can press both with one thumb to remove groups from a selection (943745, 150440); jaydik's rebind is ctrl onto the shift key, alt onto the ctrl key (544159). Pokebunny would also swap `K` and `L` to group the control groups (942105), where JimKlide finds alternating pinky and middle easier when switching groups (520960).
- Whether to remap modifiers at all, given it costs a script or a firmware flash, is itself unsettled as of 2024 (031744, 540999). See [tools-and-scripts](/tools-and-scripts.md).
- Alt and centre-on-selection, opened 2020-05-18: Mell00yell00 (terran) finds centring unnecessary when you can click the portrait, and it costs alt+repair autorepair (780113). Poletes, jaydik, Sora and Siaal want it kept, mainly for the opening sequence (251969, 876287, 509275, 395146). Compromises floated and dropped: centre on ctrl+alt, "basically unplayable" (649126); alt+middle-mouse (838047); Core Lite's alt+shift with cams on alt+waszx (828170). Double-tapping a control group versus a dedicated centre key also died (640064, 914132).

## The `/` key

- **Thumb versus index is "a pretty hot topic" (256192) and was never ruled on. The closest thing to consensus, 2021-2022, is index-primary with the thumb used to avoid double-index sequences (Edennil and woodsy88 119936, 478272)**, woodsy88 having asked the creators for a ruling and got none (478272).
- Thumb always: bro (303937), Mell00yell00 (822909), jaydik (827700, 083901), JimKlide (159444). Index wherever possible: Siaal, who "can't stand `/` on thumb" (529099). Be comfortable with both: Poletes (664992). bunfoof splits by sequence, `/k` better with the pointer (103828); Mell00yell00 thumbs everything and crosses the index over (304256). All agree the `/ .` pair, fusion core and fleet beacon, is nicer index-middle (961139).
- Edennil's argument that injecting is designed around the thumb — index, thumb, middle, thumb, ring, thumb, pinky, thumb (836146, 944990) — met ReinFórce, whose forefinger nail hits `;` and who reserves the thumb for modifiers (011634, 555645). Edennil conceded he can work around it everywhere but Zerg injects, where shifting the whole hand up is the fallback (369822, 454131). Mell00yell00: "Not sure we have had someone using 6.0 not use thumb on `/` at least sometimes" (063808). ReinFórce reads 6.0 as requiring it, build-advanced-structure having no alternate hotkey (231751). The 2020-01 version of the argument went nowhere too (690122, 225154, 596992).
- Is `/` even a good key? "the most awkward key in the core" (493225); Siaal says it eases once the index rests on semicolon, and the finger depends on the action — index for spells and units, thumb for build advanced and injects (963922, 341299).
- `/'shift` versus `/shift` for queuing an advanced building: Poletes says the extra `'` raises the ceiling, the thumb travelling right after `/` and not needing to actuate until after `'` (015980); will3285 says it's just an extra keypress against a straight thumb repetition (800020). Unsettled.

## Individual binds that never settled

- **Lift and land has had three positions in play — backspace, `]` and `=` — since 2021, with no resolution as of 2024 (200223, 803678, 350920).** ScaryMouse says it moved off `]` to backspace to stop it clashing with halt construction (385819); bunfoof disagrees, "right bracket feels like it strikes the balance of accessible and not misclickable", and notes the spreadsheet still shows lift there (471928, 599169). Altafen patches the Corelite collision with `Lift=5,6` (993133); ScaryMouse counters that swapping lift and land would do (783090). Heliac's edit list still had `]` (566835) after Mell00yell00 recanted for Backspace (739580). Lift and land on one finger was raised as repetition and answered by JaKaTaK — "Finger repetition in lift/land sequence is offset by delay between lift and land" — accepted by the asker, never independently confirmed (919977).
- Supply depot raise and lower, swap or not (007189)? JaKaTaK: "opinion seems pretty split atm" (919977). Spreadsheet and shipped file say `K` raises (999489, 548648); Mell00yell00 inverts it, `K` being the physically lower key (179124).
- The banished move command: TheCore deliberately removed a dedicated move key, Edennil now says he'd never fully banish a key again if redesigning (934045, 415084), and others still argue for restoring it (500309, 717630).
- Idle worker on `6`: wanted, but no agreement on which command to give up for it (558336, 772123).
- Warp gate is absent from v6 as race-specific, many protoss play without it, and it can't go on index without repetition against the unit keys — add it yourself (Siaal 711744, 643733). Warpgate on the back mouse button was agreed better and approved by JaKaTaK for 6 (838138) but wasn't in the shipped file as of 2020-07, and Shift+back versus plain back is unresolved (973125). Useful regardless: hold shift while rapid-firing warp-ins and the game selects the zealot once and spams locations instead of alternating select and place, about twice as fast, releasing shift dropping the pointer (618378, 219974).
- Uproot spine and spore on `-` is probably wrong: Siaal thinks it belongs on the `J ;` toggle pair and doesn't know how it got onto minus (624431); JimKlide guesses it stops you unburrowing spines when you box them with the army (853436); ScaryMouse counters that box select doesn't grab buildings with units (613322).
- Baneling detonation: marin and Schoggi want `K` (292701, 980736); ScaryMouse objects and suggests `[` (184074, 019285).
- Mothership Mass Recall (`P`) versus Nexus Recall (`K`): Hoplite proposed unifying on `K` for learnability (864312, 875164) and got no reply. Heliac's consistency proposals — the same recall move (135110), observer speed to `P` (271741), the banshee-speed / raven-energy swap (019206), lurker resume attack to `-` (569330) — drew discussion but no ruling.
- Creep queens on `M` or `I`: Mutaller experimented, no consensus (337034, 731468, 117003).
- Load on the command centre is far out on purpose: "Load CC is a really bad fat finger. We push that out far so you don't hit it on accident" (919977). Occasionally questioned, never changed.
- Rapid-fire priority after `P`: Siaal says the `[ - /` order is controversial and he doesn't remember the default (043029). Mules rapid-fire: unnecessary (935967); already are in v6 (984833); addable if you want (468103).
- Terran Select Builder on `C`: jaydik dislikes it, no formal proposal (467458, 025876). Where ping belongs in 6.0: raised, floated, never settled (226197). Swapping `K` and `H`: Remko proposes, Siaal argues against on finger load (781, 782-789); no change.
- Unresolved and unanswered: whether siege and unsiege can share one key (146818, 890324, 172830); why parasitic bomb is on `\` and not `;` (012471); why there's no "select all larva" when protoss has select all warp gates (068993, 999046).
- Does a double-tap on a build key cost anything at a high repeat rate? JimKlide is skeptical (372858); Siaal and Edennil say yes (205588, 436161).
- "Jump to last alert" is unreliable and nobody found a fix. bro: a creep tumor finishing 0.1s after you're attacked pans you to the tumor, and the only alternative is multi-tapping to the alert you want (833856, 357958); pros don't lean on it, seeing the attack roughly 80% of the time via base cams, groups and minimap (507537); bro would still train it on a macro map where a unit attacks every few seconds (155435). The 2021 restatement is the same, with "click the minimap or use camera locations instead" (768286, 177866).
- Left hanging: a terran who builds Thors and occasionally lifts every factory instead (980511), and subgroup switching without usable mouse side buttons (693344).

Rotterdam is the named high-level exception (387486), and Scarlett uses it only to ctrl-select new phoenixes into a group (131390).

## Race-specific variants, Core Lite and hybrids

- **Race-specific versions were asked for in 2020 and never came back.** Poletes, 2020-05-18: "zerg absolutely cannot have cams on shift and toss absolutely needs that" (224470, 298984), with no counterargument recorded. The earlier version is gcask's, playing Terran: "I do feel like Core 6 is zerg-targeted" (180672), because he wants jump-to-location on the shift layer for the shift-click-back-to-minerals pattern (803586). Edennil says that's impossible — "Jump to location has to either be no modifier or just control for zerg injects" (436609) — and concedes the shape of it: "Previous versions were bad for zerg macro. This sounds like it might be bad for terran macro" (829441). Parked as race-specific customisation (300330).
- Core Lite for a lighter pinky is contested. Kykeon prefers Lite (475183); cthulhug finds it puts too much on spacebar, which Brood War taught him to read as "jump to emergency" (348756); both agree grid is worse (496760, 937139). For a player missing part of his left pinky (844201), Sora and Mell00yell00 suggested Lite as least pinky-involved (077403, 820722), but Edennil disagrees, Lite pressing modifiers with the pinky, a bigger strain a short pinky worsens (696316, 500874); ScaryMouse says try it in an empty custom rather than judge from the layout picture (764648). Lite's injects needing a held shift are a defect to Edennil (401602, 005831) and a design choice to Altafen (183653). Never answered: whether Lite is the left-handed version (806017, 468021), and whether Lite is maintained the way the Juuce file is (524969); a control group guide (985482) and a visual layout (856465) for Lite were requested and never produced.
- A grid/Core hybrid, so campaign, co-op and arcade never need rebinding, was proposed by Moss (090809) and steered toward "grid for lefties with control groups layered underneath" (202183). The blocker: Shift as the add-to-CG modifier collides with Shift for queuing — queue a forge then a pylon and you accidentally add to the group on `O` (720499). Siaal's untested fix is to layer only the rare groups (741181).
- Shifting the whole layout one key left (HUIO instead of JIOP) now that cameras are on Alt: SPARROW's proposal (913537, 140787); Edennil says only if your hands are large enough to still reach Control and Shift (173972, 929216). A customisation, not a default change. See [layouts-and-variants](/layouts-and-variants.md).

## Onboarding: start on Core, or learn standard first

- **Unresolved, and restated every year.** Edennil: start on Core, "might as well not know a good hotkey layout rather than have to not know the hotkey layout twice" (783420), with Lyon agreeing that early is better if you dislike standard (423360). Laaxus argues the opposite through 2024-07 — play standard or a custom grid for a week or two, maybe a month, and come back when committed (811059, 325504, 173286, 038268) — because "most pro don't use thecore afaik" (672936, 427390) and "you don't need thecore to reach high level" (465887).
- Is the right side of the keyboard worth relearning at all? Pokebunny, 2020: "this isn't even just retraining the way i play starcraft, this is retraining the way i use a keyboard" (775007). Answers: ten control groups and eight camera locations under your fingers (083452); standard's layering guarantees finger repetition eventually (722965); the left side is only the default elsewhere because casual and laptop players couldn't manage the right (643679).
- Is TheCourse usable by a complete beginner? Coyotebd says it's shorthand aimed at people who already played a while — "Pick a precise inject method" isn't newbie-facing, and you're forgetting the other resources you used alongside it (292245, 153566).
