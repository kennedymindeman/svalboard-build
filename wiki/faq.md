# FAQ

Message ids in parentheses are the last 6 digits of Discord snowflakes in `discord/raw/channel-1124364902811844739.jsonl`; a few 6-digit suffixes collide in the 43k-message export, so disambiguate by date if a lookup returns two hits. Agreement counts are floor estimates.

Where an answer changed over the three years, the entry leads with the current one and dates the shift.

## Is it for me?

- **Do you type faster on a Sval?** (547952, 799, 249080, 397455, 151125) → No. Buy for pain relief;
  expect equal-or-slower burst speed, far lower effort and much higher endurance (806, 278221). Steady
  since 2023: claussen ~90+ wpm and JesusFreke ~120 at the top (479610, 069221), phreaker 60-70
  (307329), long-run loss "generally equal from what I've seen" (663082, 962928).
- **How long until I'm proficient, and why do my fingers cramp?** (557854, 868041, 955462, 721060,
  826442) → One week to one month; 1-2 weeks from a split with thumb clusters (225925, 307530),
  near-zero from a Datahand (358532). 11-14 wpm on day one is normal; cramping means you're hovering
  instead of resting on center/south — fix the palm rest, give it two weeks. claussen, phreaker
  (815232, 076309, 361595).
- **Will it actually fix my RSI?** (703710, 519104, 381826) → Depends where the pain starts. The
  Datahand architecture "reduces workload by a huge fraction"; relief is dramatic for hands and
  fingers, neutral above the wrist unless you also tent and mount, and claussen says confirm the
  diagnosis (452648, 245544, 835497, 697050). See [ergonomics-and-fit](/ergonomics-and-fit.md).
- **Sval vs Glove80?** (986945, 206411, 528063) → Different families: Glove80 continues the Kinesis
  line, Sval the Datahand; wired-only, and the 20 g switches plus integrated pointing are the selling
  point (665494, 718202). phreaker has used both: Glove80 lets you avoid layers, the Sval will not, but
  its switches and ergonomics are "a world better" (651698, 930676).
- **Sval vs Datahand vs lalboard?** (383370, 848862, 971524) → vs Datahand: "the mechanisms are almost
  identical in feel, but the fit is another universe", plus repairability and QMK (105482, 538993). vs
  lalboard: a product "designed to fit any person easily" against "a project under occasional
  development". claussen (905536, 817344).
- **Can I try one before buying?** (983143, 623498, 555851, 824834) → The $15 sample cluster SKU, the
  trial program (up to three months), or ask claussen to matchmake a local owner; he and phreaker both
  broker introductions (061020, 017704, 678929).
- **Can I game on it?** (464052, 503233, 933383) → Yes for RPG/ARPG and most shooters, no for Osu or
  frame-perfect play; keep a base gaming layer and rebind WASD with W off the north key. Raven System,
  phreaker (426264, 583319, 754446). See [gaming](/gaming.md).
## Buying, price and shipping

- **Kit or prebuilt?** (976691, 648942, 151051) → If you're in pain or new to printing, buy the full
  build; the kit is for people who *want* to print, and prebuilt is cheaper unless you value your time
  very low. Lightly vs Panda is colour only. claussen, phreaker (320835, 313334, 905647).
- **Is the self-print kit going away? What are lead times?** (990684, 239958, 913530, 097940, 103367)
  → Batches, not EOL; product pages carry current lead times, which moved from 2-3 to 4-5 weeks in
  December 2025 (265113, 819180, 579660). Prebuilts historically ran ~1-2 weeks of build and kit
  batches were announced a month ahead. claussen, Invi (009958, 213205, 683679).
- **Is there a Black Friday sale or discount code?** (710036, 654346, 830292) → No. Svalboard runs no
  sales, ever (616842, 189544). No real used market either — price doesn't move, though test rigs and
  "mules" get offered in-channel. claussen, phreaker (679957, 723305).
- **Return policy / can I resell mine?** (451262, 625) → Kits are not returnable; prebuilts per site
  policy; reselling self-print kit builds for profit is discouraged (757222, 1243). The prebuilt trial
  policy is deliberately generous. claussen (352241).
- **Does it ship internationally, and are duties included?** (116106, 969483, 390397, 880402) → Yes:
  California by UPS, 3-5 days domestic, ~3 days to central Europe. Prices are duty-inclusive through
  Shopify Managed Markets, UK VAT included, EU packages customs pre-cleared; India is the outlier.
  claussen, hii (655886, 244925, 083211).

## Fit, comfort and health

- **Do I have to measure my fingertips, and what if the size is wrong?** (963860, 749556, 948725,
  088062, 738890, 439091) → Yes, and trust the sizer: it only sets NSEW key thickness, those keys swap
  out, and thickness isn't adjustable afterwards; round up when borderline (500507, 052999, 931812).
  Measure every finger resting flat, plus thumb *widths* not tips, and put all the numbers in the order
  instructions; 14 and 15 mm share a key. Stock fits roughly 6-7" hands and the narrow clusters cover
  fits below 16-17 mm. claussen, iynaix, Cyrus (908126, 679987, 353162, 609428, 176532, 896594).
- **How heavy are the keys, and can I change the weight?** (029821, 356244, 251274, 936491) → ~20 gf,
  front-loaded, "zero movement until breakaway" — not a 20 g linear. Force is set at print time by
  magnet offset (0.7 mm ≈ 20 gf standard, zero offset 65 g+); no software adjustment, and MX centers
  are a hard no. claussen, phreaker (860788, 082972, 672924, 023131, 178001).
- **Do I need palm rests, and do I rest on them while typing?** (128029, 811467, 915460, 214017) → Yes,
  resting is encouraged; a few go without, but learn with one — they also cut false hits on the lateral
  keys. Cyrus, claussen (028454, 923424, 167254).
- **Aren't the lateral (E/W) keys bad for you, and can I use vim?** (253372, 029555, 533427) → Only
  outward index and middle are new motions; they're 2-5 mm and learnable ("do spocks"), and you can
  leave them unmapped — one return ever over lateral pain. Vim is fine on the arrows. claussen,
  phreaker (183424, 716181, 060736).
- **How do I mount or tent it — my desk is the wrong height?** (880850, 652472, 488346, 318696) → 5° in
  the case, ~15° on the towers, 20-30° on M5 legs, arbitrary off the 1/4-20 thread; the stock rig is
  SmallRig rosette arms with a CAMVATE clamp. claussen, Diitsuku (259624, 735730, 841589).
## Printing and the self-print kit

- **Do I need a printer? Can a service, library or makerspace do it?** (303461, 733861, 875436, 244133,
  130764, 826400) → No third-party service will hold the tolerances — "nobody will guarantee 20ths of a
  millimetre" — and the license is explicit; buy a used A1 mini or P1S, use a makerspace, or buy
  prebuilt: "Nobody regrets buying a pre-built" (382096, 666782, 817, 660337, 835631). A friend's
  printer works only if you can iterate together. claussen, phreaker (106465).
- **Where do I get the print files?** (178522, 575536, 932157, 408057, 925760, 855299, 474071) → A
  private GitHub repo, STEP rather than STL now (730007). The invite email usually lands in spam, or
  hides at github.com/orgs/svalboard/invitation. Access is customer-only (prebuilt buyers included) and
  granted in a manual ~weekly pass — give claussen or lumbduck your order number, or run `!access`
  (466778, 193381, 739200, 477551, 264993, 027072). Hand-run since 2023 (108820).
- **How hard is the self-print kit, and does it need soldering?** (736176, 343487, 432138) → No
  soldering, though heat-set inserts need a soldering *iron*; assembly is "tinker toys and glue" and
  magnet polarity is the fiddly part. Printing is the hard half: calibration cubes, retraction towers,
  0.05-0.1 mm repeatable tolerances. claussen, beep (769664, 097630, 106142). claussen's 2023-09 summary
  has not needed revising: "It's about as complicated as it gets in terms of precision printing, but it's
  a different kind of complicated than hand-wiring a dactyl. Lots of little fiddly magnet gluing. No
  soldering. Plenty of printing and cleaning up of prints unless your stuff is super dialed. But there
  are pretty good fixtures for making most of it straightforward" (487208), alongside a Google Slides
  build guide (233229). On the print list: **eight distinct key shapes**, in counts of 8, 24, 2, 2, 2, 2,
  1 and 1 (796803).
- **What material, and can I resin-print?** (681330, 949109, 136396, 235453, 839017) → FDM, plain PLA
  (ABS supported too, PETG for some); resin is out — not skin-safe, brittle (740672, 191973, 031350,
  851457). ABS buys durability, PLA is cheaper and thermoforms the palm rest, parts are interchangeable;
  keep filament dry, skip CF/GF and silk. claussen, whitelynx (599817, 074580). See
  [printing](/printing.md).
- **Does filament colour matter?** (778890, 932767, 614770) → Yes, for the clusters: everything but
  verified blacks is IR-transparent (eSun/Prusa/Galaxy black are tested), so silver-sharpie every flag
  and consider inking the PCB's center optos. Case colour is free. claussen (639296, 001717, 052222).
- **How much filament, and will it fit my printer?** (952096, 206411, 227412, 668628, 949983, 456151)
  → Under 1 kg for both halves; Bambu P1S or X1C is the reference; the A1 mini needs the case split
  (665494, 793783, 093888). Measured totals in 2024-25 ran 410-500 g (524497, 981362). The baseplate is
  the constraint at ~200 mm, and a well-dialed Ender 3 has been done. claussen (316837, 272081).
- **What magnets and bearings do I need, and do I buy switches?** (090634, 371963, 706992, 112458,
  291935) → N35 2 mm × 1.5 mm magnets; 1/8" (3.175 mm) G5 ZrO2 bearings, 3-4 per side (663048, 865893,
  636353). No switches — the board is optical, magnets interrupt a beam. Polarity only has to be
  self-consistent unless you ordered preassembled magnet parts. Raven System (344510, 395342). **To tell
  which pole is which**, use a phone compass or gaussmeter app — claussen uses keuwlsoft's on Android —
  and hold the magnet very close, since it is "very near field"; or skip measuring and let an
  already-installed magnet be the reference, attracting the next one to find which face goes out
  (874277, 305444, 268557, 728037, 710016). Factory and replacement parts follow the build guide's
  convention: **S pole out on keys, N pole out on clusters** — the guide's wording as quoted by two
  builders, with claussen confirming "I ship the described polarity only" (112458, 911771, 446664,
  876723). A 2023 claussen message said the inverse, "N-up keys and S-up clusters" (315570); the
  discrepancy was never flagged in-channel, and "up" there may describe the assembly jig rather than
  the finished part's exposed face. See
  [suppliers-and-parts](/suppliers-and-parts.md).
- **V1 vs V2 printed parts — do I reprint?** (762481, 905628, 284424, 898728) → Print 2.0: V2
  keys/clusters aren't cross-compatible with V1 plastics, but PCBAs and hardware are unchanged (957603,
  877762). Earlier versioning went the same way — v1.1b baseplates, then v1.2 as the default with
  tipping clusters and deprecated sliders. claussen (394304, 348826, 518727).
## Pointing devices

- **Does the self-print kit include trackballs?** (082059, 632544, 915290, 626826, 299941) → No.
  "+2x Trackball PCBAs" means sensor boards only — source ball, bearings, glue and a silver sharpie
  yourself; prebuilts include them (541298, 241600, 581450). Same answer in every window since 2024.
  claussen, Raven System (678658, 953368).
- **Which ball size?** (971998, 393842, 213882, 313253, 482703, 045571) → 44 mm. 52 needs hands over
  20 cm, compromises fit and wants custom holders (984528, 628269). The default walked up: 34 mm in
  early 2024 (438785), 40 mm through late 2024 with 44 called "a bit big" (526602, 313444), 44 mm
  standard from October 2024 on, ~55 mm the ceiling. The printed holder, not the PCB, sets size.
  claussen, phreaker (454874, 726181, 142999).
- **Where do I buy the ball and the bearings?** (934224, 744584, 746505) → 3.175 mm G5 zirconia
  statics — uxcell on Amazon, `B081SPLWT5` — and a Sanwa-style ball: red anywhere, silver from
  Svalboard. claussen, Invi (573341, 136032, 647407).
- **One trackball or two? Which pointing device for RSI?** (641007, 024660, 355742, 016064, 899442,
  351926) → Two: one to point, one to scroll; swappable later (296465, 135043, 575685). Dual ball has
  been the enthusiast answer since mid-2024; ball-plus-trackpad wins if clicking hurts (glass Azoteq
  TPS43, no OS gestures), and the trackpoint keeps a niche for tiny, low-force motion under the middle
  finger. claussen, phreaker (263346, 660509, 940341, 732261). See [pointing-devices](/pointing-devices.md).
- **Can I add or swap a pointing device later?** (805388, 274581, 085537, 443661) → Yes, any time and
  non-destructively — "just plugging in a cable and reflashing": six screws, an FFC latch, a printed
  holder and a sensor PCBA. A trackpad needs a non-alpha PCB, and third-party pointers won't integrate
  with the mouse keys. claussen (762516, 448467, 200339, 770297).
- **How do I make one ball scroll?** (839685, 514576, 555854) → Bind `Scroll Left/Right Toggle` on a
  non-mouse layer, press it once on the board, then unbind; it persists in EEPROM. See
  svalboard.com/scroll. Cyrus, claussen (460254, 403051, 898660).
- **How do I click, get out of the mouse layer, and change the DPI?** (048775, 779270, 795782, 124410)
  → Moving a ball auto-activates layer 15; click with the opposite hand's south keys, remappable, and
  exit with any non-mouse key or a transparent `MO()`. "Mouse key timer" sets the timeout (default
  500); raise DPI on the board (max 2400), not in the OS. claussen, phreaker (886423, 858078, 852166,
  941510).

## Firmware, configuration and connectivity

- **Does it ship with firmware, and what programs it?** (688121, 569, 1166, 387072) → No, POST only;
  flash the latest vial-qmk release, then use Keybard (Vial or vial.rocks also work for plain keymap edits, but Keybard is the current tool; see [firmware-and-config](/firmware-and-config.md)) (450890, 992539, 311742).
  It's vial-qmk on a custom branch — fork, push a tag, GitHub Actions builds it. phreaker (182933,
  070785). See [firmware-and-config](/firmware-and-config.md).
- **Do I need Vial or any software running?** (426578, 380431, 269245, 883728) → No: the keymap lives
  in flash, so afterwards it's an ordinary USB HID device and a locked-down work PC, an Android phone
  or a plain USB 1.1 port all work. Build the layout before the board arrives from Keybard's examples
  (the Vial desktop app errors out). phreaker, CwD (812136, 155399, 142657).
- **How do I flash it?** (416906, 404298, 029674) → Back up your `.vil`, USB into the U port,
  double-tap the reset button underneath, drag the uf2 onto the drive that appears. Each half flashes
  separately with its matching variant; keymap-only changes usually need only the master. phreaker,
  claussen (297685, 341864, 654066).
- **How do I save my layout, and is there a factory reset?** (376192, 199564, 916446) → Already saved —
  Vial writes EEPROM live. Worst case, double-tap RESET into the bootloader and reload a `.vil` from
  svalboard-configs; ~10 layers and ~100 of everything else fit. claussen (493380, 600715, 537428).
- **How do numbers, symbols, modifiers and arrows work?** (634880, 973393, 554773, 255508) → One NAS
  layer on right-thumb-down puts numbers on the home row and symbols on the North row; arrows default
  to index-finger NSEW. Modifiers ride the thumbs — claussen holds control+shift+tab with one — plus
  home-row mods or meh/hyper; the "home row" is each cluster's center key. phreaker, Jaboo36 (683620,
  356584, 976182, 833546).
- **How do the two halves connect, and is the cable proprietary?** (899850, 450236, 592230) → Plain
  USB-C: peripheral half to central, central to host, recognised as an ordinary keyboard, no driver.
  The kit ships the C-C cable; you supply the host cable. ccotenj, Cyrus (669909, 197097, 077342).
- **Is there a wireless option?** (991464, 376303, 302122) → No, and probably never: the sensor LEDs
  pull ~300-500 mW, QMK and the non-USB inter-half link both block it, and ZMK has no split-pointing
  support. People bridge with a handheldsci.com/kb, HD-600 or BT600 adapter plus a power bank.
  claussen, phreaker (833546, 583647, 328257). The answer is unchanged since 2023-08, when claussen had
  already run one on "a $50 USB-BT adapter and a big battery pack, with the split connection still
  wired", getting "at least a few days out of a big 10000mAh battery pack" — the target being
  chair-mounting, not portability, because "Full wireless split is a whole other pot" (501051). See [open-questions](/open-questions.md).
- **Is it open source?** (402625, 989697, 198997, 580497) → Split: software is FOSS (vial-qmk),
  mechanical solid models are open to customers — "free as in speech, not as in beer" — and the
  electronics stay closed because they're optical and subminiature. STEP yes, OnShape no, with a
  commitment to open everything if the company folds. claussen (704192, 692496, 526666, 532718).

## Troubleshooting

- **My board is stuck repeating one character.** (516097) → Find the key in the matrix view, check for
  a loose key or cluster magnet, blow out debris; a cracked opto-interrupter trace or failed magnet is
  possible, and a pulled key autofires because the switches are normally-closed (013242, 965436).
- **A key registers as always-on straight off the printer.** (819856) → Print defect in the key well;
  bevel the front lower edge. Cyrus, claussen (083776, 738438).
- **Keys trigger the wrong character, or Windows doesn't see the board.** (936465, 678948, 486252) →
  Your filament isn't IR-opaque; silver-sharpie every flag. claussen, Cyrus (562608, 262474).
- **All my keys stopped working mid-typing.** (165457, 134617, 970984) → You hit right thumb up and
  are on the function layer; left thumb up returns to base (015400, 801298).
- **My Mac or hub only sees some keys.** (188244, 391946, 083668) → Plug straight into the machine and
  give the hub real power; use Vial's matrix tester to rule out a stuck key. phreaker, claussen
  (746335, 511250).
- **The thumb keys look greyed out / where's the second click?** (775845, 247454, 231591) → Press the
  thumb center past its travel and a tact switch clicks; it can't be bound separately from the first.
  Raven System, claussen (421801, 166705, 918569).
- **My trackball doesn't trigger the mouse layer.** (653310, 946188, 237845) → See
  svalboard.com/scroll and check you haven't hit the automouse toggle (541826, 826847).
