# Svalboard knowledge base

Build and configuration knowledge for the Svalboard in this repo, as an [OKF v0.2](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md) bundle. Everything here is sourced so far from the distillation of the Svalboard Discord #general channel — see [discord/README.md](../discord/README.md) for provenance and for how to extend the coverage backwards or to other channels. Record changes in [log.md](/log.md); the log is oldest-first.

## Overview

- [discord-general-overview](/discord-general-overview.md) — What the Svalboard Discord #general channel is, who the recurring voices are, and what the distillation does not cover.

## Building the board

- [printing](/printing.md) — Self-print material, printer, dimensional-accuracy, bed/slicer, glue and tooling recommendations.
- [pointing-devices](/pointing-devices.md) — Trackball size and sourcing, bearings versus BTUs, trackpad and trackpoint limits, and friction fixes.
- [ergonomics-and-fit](/ergonomics-and-fit.md) — Fit order, cluster geometry, key weight, palm rests, mounting, and fixes for specific pains.
- [suppliers-and-parts](/suppliers-and-parts.md) — Part numbers and sources for magnets, bearings, balls, BTUs, cables, adapters and accessories.

## Using the board

- [firmware-and-config](/firmware-and-config.md) — Which firmware release to run, Keybard versus Vial, mouse-layer and pointing settings, and the flashing gotchas.
- [gaming](/gaming.md) — Gaming-layer setup, which genres work on trackballs, and WASD placement.

## Reference

- [faq](/faq.md) — The newcomer questions asked more than once, with the channel's answers.
- [timeline](/timeline.md) — Dated Svalboard product news, policy and firmware announcements from November 2025 to August 2026.
- [open-questions](/open-questions.md) — Contested or unresolved topics — wireless, BTUs versus statics, armrests, layout resets, wake-from-sleep.
- [links](/links.md) — Official pages, code and CAD repos, config tools, videos and parts sources cited in the channel.
